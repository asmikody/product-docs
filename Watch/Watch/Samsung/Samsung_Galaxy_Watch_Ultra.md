# Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra

## Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra Basic Information

• Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra is a Samsung smartwatch launched in July 2024.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra uses a 47 mm titanium case.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra provides 32 GB of storage.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra uses a 1.5-inch Super AMOLED display with 480 x 480 resolution.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra uses sapphire crystal protection.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra supports dual-frequency GPS.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra supports LTE connectivity.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra supports heart-rate monitoring, sleep tracking and body-composition measurements.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra has 10ATM water resistance and IP68 protection.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra supports up to 100 hours in Power Saving mode under Samsung's stated conditions.
