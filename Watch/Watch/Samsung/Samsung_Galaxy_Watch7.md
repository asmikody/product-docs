# Samsung Galaxy Watch7

## Samsung Galaxy Watch7 Basic Information

• Samsung Galaxy Watch7 is a Samsung smartwatch launched in July 2024.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch7 is available in 40 mm and 44 mm sizes.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch7 uses a Super AMOLED display with sapphire crystal protection.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch7 supports dual-frequency GPS.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch7 supports heart-rate monitoring, sleep tracking, blood oxygen monitoring and body-composition measurements.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch7 supports ECG-related measurements where available.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch7 supports Bluetooth 5.3, Wi-Fi, GPS and NFC.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch7 is available in Bluetooth and LTE variants.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch7 supports 5ATM water resistance and IP68 protection.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch7 supports MIL-STD-810H durability.
