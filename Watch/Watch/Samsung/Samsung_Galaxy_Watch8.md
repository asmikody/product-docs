# Samsung Galaxy Watch8

## Samsung Galaxy Watch8 Basic Information

• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 is a Samsung Galaxy smartwatch introduced in July 2025.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 is available in 40 mm and 44 mm sizes.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 runs Wear OS Powered by Samsung.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 is designed for everyday health and fitness tracking.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 has 40 mm and 44 mm case options.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 uses an aluminum-based case construction.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 has an approximately 8.6 mm thin profile.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 supports interchangeable bands.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 has 5ATM water resistance and IP68 protection.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 supports MIL-STD-810H durability.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 uses a Super AMOLED display.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 provides a 438 x 438 resolution in the 40 mm version.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 provides a 480 x 480 resolution in the 44 mm version.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 supports an Always On Display.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch8 supports touchscreen interaction.
