# Samsung Galaxy Watch6 Classic

## Samsung Galaxy Watch6 Classic Basic Information

• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 Classic is a Samsung smartwatch launched in July 2023.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 Classic is available in 43 mm and 47 mm sizes.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 Classic uses a stainless-steel case and rotating bezel.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 Classic uses a Super AMOLED display with sapphire crystal protection.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 Classic supports heart-rate monitoring, ECG-related measurements, blood oxygen monitoring and sleep tracking.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 Classic supports body-composition measurements.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 Classic supports GPS, Bluetooth 5.3, Wi-Fi and NFC.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 Classic is available in Bluetooth and LTE variants.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 Classic has 5ATM water resistance and IP68 protection.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 Classic supports MIL-STD-810H durability.
