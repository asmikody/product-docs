# Samsung Galaxy Watch6

## Samsung Galaxy Watch6 Basic Information

• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 is a Samsung smartwatch launched in July 2023.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 is available in 40 mm and 44 mm sizes.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 uses an aluminum case and Super AMOLED display.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 uses sapphire crystal protection.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 supports heart-rate monitoring, ECG-related measurements, blood oxygen monitoring and sleep tracking.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 supports body-composition measurements.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 supports GPS, Bluetooth 5.3, Wi-Fi and NFC.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 is available in Bluetooth and LTE variants.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 has 5ATM water resistance and IP68 protection.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch6 supports MIL-STD-810H durability.
