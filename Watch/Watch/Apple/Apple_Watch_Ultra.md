# Apple Watch Ultra

## Apple Watch Ultra — Product Overview

- Apple Watch Ultra is an Apple Watch model introduced in 2022.
- Apple Watch Ultra is designed for communication, health, fitness, safety and everyday smartwatch use.
- Apple Watch Ultra is available in 49 mm case sizes.
- Apple Watch Ultra uses a Titanium case construction.
- Apple Watch Ultra is powered by the S8 SiP.
- Apple Watch Ultra runs Apple's watchOS platform.
- Apple Watch Ultra is offered in GPS and/or GPS + Cellular configurations depending on the model and market.
- Apple Watch Ultra feature availability can vary by region, configuration, connected iPhone and software version.
