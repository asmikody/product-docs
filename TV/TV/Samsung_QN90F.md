# Samsung QN90F


## 1. Samsung QN90F Overview
• Samsung QN90F is a Neo QLED 4K smart television from Samsung's 2025 lineup.
• Samsung QN90F uses model number QA75QN90FAUXXL for the 75-inch India-market variant documented here.
• Samsung QN90F is available in multiple screen sizes across the 2025 series.
• Samsung QN90F runs on Samsung Tizen Smart TV software with Samsung Vision AI features.
