# Samsung Q7F


## 1. Samsung Q7F Overview
• Samsung Q7F is a QLED 4K smart television from Samsung's 2025 lineup.
• Samsung Q7F uses model number QA55Q7FAAULXL for the 55-inch India-market variant documented here.
• Samsung Q7F is available in multiple screen sizes in the 2025 series.
• Samsung Q7F runs on Samsung Tizen Smart TV software with Samsung Vision AI features.

## 2. Samsung Q7F Display and Picture Quality
• Samsung Q7F uses QLED display technology with Quantum Dot color reproduction.
• Samsung Q7F provides 4K resolution at 3840 × 2160 pixels.
• Samsung Q7F supports Quantum HDR and HDR10+.
• Samsung Q7F supports Motion Xcelerator for compatible motion processing.
• Samsung Q7F supports 4K upscaling for compatible lower-resolution sources.
• Samsung Q7F supports 100% Color Volume with Quantum Dot technology as specified by Samsung.
• Samsung Q7F supports Supreme UHD Dimming, Mega Contrast and Color Booster Pro.

## 3. Samsung Q7F Smart TV Features

• Samsung Q7F supports Apple AirPlay, screen mirroring, Tap View and multi-device features.
• Samsung Q7F supports Samsung SmartThings Hub and compatible smart-home functions.
• Samsung Q7F includes Samsung Knox security features for supported smart-TV services.
