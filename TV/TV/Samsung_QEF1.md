# Samsung QEF1


## 1. Samsung QEF1 Overview
• Samsung QEF1 is a QLED 4K smart television from Samsung's 2025 lineup.
• Samsung QEF1 uses model number QA75QEF1AUXXL for the 75-inch India-market variant documented here.
• Samsung QEF1 is available in multiple screen sizes depending on regional configuration.
• Samsung QEF1 runs on Samsung Tizen Smart TV software with Samsung Vision AI features.

## 2. Samsung QEF1 Display and Picture Quality
• Samsung QEF1 uses QLED display technology with Quantum Dot color reproduction.
• Samsung QEF1 provides 4K resolution at 3840 × 2160 pixels.
• Samsung QEF1 supports Quantum HDR and HDR10+.
• Samsung QEF1 supports Motion Xcelerator for compatible motion processing.
• Samsung QEF1 supports 4K upscaling for compatible lower-resolution content.
• Samsung QEF1 supports 100% Color Volume with Quantum Dot technology as specified by Samsung.
• Samsung QEF1 supports Supreme UHD Dimming, Mega Contrast and Color Booster Pro.

• Samsung QEF1 includes Samsung Knox security features for supported smart-TV services.
