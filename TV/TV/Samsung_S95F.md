# Samsung S95F


## 1. Samsung S95F Overview
• Samsung S95F is an OLED 4K smart television from Samsung's 2025 lineup.
• Samsung S95F uses model number QA83S95FAEXXL for the 83-inch India-market variant documented here.
• Samsung S95F is available in 55-inch, 65-inch, 77-inch and 83-inch class sizes in the 2025 series.
• Samsung S95F runs on Samsung Tizen Smart TV software with Samsung Vision AI features.

## 2. Samsung S95F Display and Picture Quality
• Samsung S95F uses an OLED display with self-illuminating pixels.
• Samsung S95F provides 4K resolution at 3840 × 2160 pixels.
• Samsung S95F supports OLED HDR Pro and HDR10+ Adaptive and Gaming.
• Samsung S95F supports a Motion Xcelerator 165Hz mode on compatible content and devices.
• Samsung S95F supports 4K AI Upscaling Pro for compatible lower-resolution content.
• Samsung S95F includes Glare Free screen treatment to reduce reflections.
• Samsung S95F supports Ultra Viewing Angle for compatible viewing positions.
