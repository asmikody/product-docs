# Xiaomi TV FX Mini LED 55

• Xiaomi TV FX Mini LED 55 is a 55-inch QD Mini LED 4K smart television in Xiaomi's FX Mini LED series.
• Xiaomi TV FX Mini LED 55 provides 3840 × 2160 4K resolution.
• Xiaomi TV FX Mini LED 55 uses Fire TV as the smart-TV platform.

• Xiaomi TV FX Mini LED 55 supports HDR10+, HLG and Filmmaker Mode.
