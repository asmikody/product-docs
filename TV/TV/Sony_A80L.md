# Sony A80L

## 1. Sony A80L Overview

- Sony A80L is a OLED 4K Google TV.
- Sony A80L uses the model number XR-55A80L.
- Sony A80L was released for the 2023 model year.
- Sony A80L is documented in Sony India support material.

## 2. Sony A80L Display and Picture Quality

- Sony A80L is available with a 55 inches (54.6 inches measured diagonally) display.
- Sony A80L uses OLED display technology.
- Sony A80L supports a 3840 × 2160 pixels resolution.
- Sony A80L supports a 100/120 Hz panel refresh rate.
- Sony A80L supports HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision for HDR content.
- Sony A80L uses the Cognitive Processor XR.
- Sony A80L features XR OLED Contrast Pro, XR HDR Remaster, Pixel Contrast Booster, XR TRILUMINOS Pro and XR Clear Image.

## 3. Sony A80L Smart TV Features

- Sony A80L runs on Google TV.
- Sony A80L supports Google TV, Google Play Store, Google Cast, Apple AirPlay, Apple HomeKit and Sony Pictures Core.
- Sony A80L supports Google Assistant voice search with a built-in microphone.
- Sony A80L supports mobile casting and screen mirroring through supported Google Cast or Apple AirPlay features.

## 4. Sony A80L Audio and Sound

- Sony A80L uses Acoustic Surface Audio+ with Actuator ×3 and Subwoofer ×2.
- Sony A80L provides 50 W of rated audio output.
- Sony A80L supports Dolby Atmos.
- Sony A80L has the following DTS specification: DTS Digital Surround.
- Sony A80L features Room and User Position Compensation and Acoustic Surface Audio+ processing.

## 5. Sony A80L Connectivity and Ports

- Sony A80L includes 4 HDMI inputs.
- Sony A80L supports 4K 120 Hz, eARC, VRR and ALLM.
- Sony A80L includes 2 USB ports.
- Sony A80L includes 1 digital audio output.
- Sony A80L includes 1 LAN input.
- Sony A80L supports Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 4.2.

## 6. Sony A80L Gaming Features

- Sony A80L supports VRR on HDMI 3/4, ALLM on HDMI 3/4 and 4K 120 Hz input.
- Sony A80L supports compatible PCs and game consoles through HDMI.
- Sony A80L supports Game picture mode for compatible gaming sources.
- Sony A80L supports PlayStation 5 picture features where specified by Sony.

## 7. Sony A80L Design and Physical Specifications

- Sony A80L has the following physical specification: 1,227 × 712 × 53 mm without stand; 1,227 × 738 × 327 mm with stand; 17.9 kg without stand and 18.8 kg with stand; VESA 300 × 300 mm.
- Sony A80L supports wall mounting through its specified VESA pattern where one is published.
- Sony A80L uses a Sony-specified table-top stand configuration.

## 8. Sony A80L Compatibility and Useful Features

- Sony A80L is compatible with HDMI-connected media players, PCs and game consoles.
- Sony A80L supports BRAVIA Sync and HDMI-CEC where specified.
- Sony A80L supports Sony accessibility features provided by its software.

## 9. Sony A80L Package and Warranty

- Sony A80L includes Table Top Stand, Voice Remote Control, batteries, AC power cord and setup documentation.
- Sony A80L is covered by the applicable Sony India warranty terms for the specific purchase and region.
