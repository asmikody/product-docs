# Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 43

## 1. Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 43 Overview
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 43 is a 43-inch QLED 4K smart television from Xiaomi India's 2025 FX Pro QLED series.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 43 provides 3840 × 2160 4K Ultra HD resolution.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 43 uses Fire TV built in as the smart-TV platform.

• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 43 supports a 60Hz refresh rate.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 43 supports HDR-compatible content and Filmmaker Mode.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 43 supports a 178-degree horizontal and vertical viewing angle.
