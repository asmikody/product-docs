# Xiaomi Smart TV A Pro QLED 32

## 1. Xiaomi Smart TV A Pro QLED 32 Overview
• Xiaomi Smart TV A Pro QLED 32 is a 32-inch QLED HD smart television from Xiaomi's A Pro QLED series.
• Xiaomi Smart TV A Pro QLED 32 provides 1366 × 768 HD resolution.
• Xiaomi Smart TV A Pro QLED 32 uses Xiaomi PatchWall smart-TV software.

• Xiaomi Smart TV A Pro QLED 32 supports a 60Hz refresh rate.
• Xiaomi Smart TV A Pro QLED 32 supports a 178-degree viewing angle.
• Xiaomi Smart TV A Pro QLED 32 supports compatible HDR content on the documented software and decoder platform.
