# Sony X90L

## 1. Sony X90L Overview

- Sony X90L is a Full Array LED 4K Google TV.
- Sony X90L uses the model number XR-55X90L.
- Sony X90L was released for the 2023 model year.
- Sony X90L is documented in Sony India support material.

## 2. Sony X90L Display and Picture Quality

- Sony X90L is available with a 55 inches (54.6 inches measured diagonally) display.
- Sony X90L uses Full Array LED LCD display technology.
- Sony X90L supports a 3840 × 2160 pixels resolution.
- Sony X90L supports a 100/120 Hz panel refresh rate.
- Sony X90L supports HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision for HDR content.
- Sony X90L uses the Cognitive Processor XR.
- Sony X90L features XR Contrast Booster 10, XR HDR Remaster, XR TRILUMINOS Pro, XR Clear Image and XR Motion Clarity.

## 3. Sony X90L Smart TV Features

- Sony X90L runs on Google TV.
- Sony X90L supports Google TV, Google Play Store, Google Cast, Apple AirPlay, Apple HomeKit and Sony Pictures Core.
- Sony X90L supports Google Assistant voice search with a built-in microphone.
- Sony X90L supports mobile casting and screen mirroring through supported Google Cast or Apple AirPlay features.

## 4. Sony X90L Audio and Sound

- Sony X90L uses Acoustic Multi-Audio with Sound Positioning Tweeter and X-Balanced Speaker.
- Sony X90L provides 30 W of rated audio output.
- Sony X90L supports Dolby Atmos.
- Sony X90L has the following DTS specification: DTS Digital Surround.
- Sony X90L features Voice Zoom 2 and Room and User Position Compensation.

## 5. Sony X90L Connectivity and Ports

- Sony X90L includes 4 HDMI inputs.
- Sony X90L supports 4K 120 Hz, eARC, VRR and ALLM.
- Sony X90L includes 2 USB ports.
- Sony X90L includes 1 digital audio output.
- Sony X90L includes 1 LAN input.
- Sony X90L supports Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 4.2.

## 6. Sony X90L Gaming Features

- Sony X90L supports VRR on HDMI 3/4, ALLM on HDMI 3/4 and 4K 120 Hz input.
- Sony X90L supports compatible PCs and game consoles through HDMI.
- Sony X90L supports Game picture mode for compatible gaming sources.
- Sony X90L supports PlayStation 5 picture features where specified by Sony.

## 7. Sony X90L Design and Physical Specifications

- Sony X90L has the following physical specification: 1,225 × 707 × 56 mm without stand; 1,225 × 738 × 345 mm with stand; 16.9 kg without stand and 17.7 kg with stand; VESA 300 × 300 mm.
- Sony X90L supports wall mounting through its specified VESA pattern where one is published.
- Sony X90L uses a Sony-specified table-top stand configuration.

## 8. Sony X90L Compatibility and Useful Features

- Sony X90L is compatible with HDMI-connected media players, PCs and game consoles.
- Sony X90L supports BRAVIA Sync and HDMI-CEC where specified.
- Sony X90L supports Sony accessibility features provided by its software.

## 9. Sony X90L Package and Warranty

- Sony X90L includes Table Top Stand, Voice Remote Control, batteries, AC power cord and setup documentation.
- Sony X90L is covered by the applicable Sony India warranty terms for the specific purchase and region.
