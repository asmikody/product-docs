# Samsung QN70F


## 1. Samsung QN70F Overview
• Samsung QN70F is a Neo QLED 4K smart television from Samsung's 2025 lineup.
• Samsung QN70F uses model number QA55QN70FAULXL for the 55-inch India-market variant documented here.
• Samsung QN70F is available in multiple screen sizes in the 2025 series.
• Samsung QN70F runs on Samsung Tizen Smart TV software with Samsung Vision AI features.

## 2. Samsung QN70F Display and Picture Quality
• Samsung QN70F uses Neo QLED display technology with Quantum Mini LED lighting.
• Samsung QN70F provides 4K resolution at 3840 × 2160 pixels.
• Samsung QN70F supports Neo Quantum HDR and HDR10+ Adaptive and Gaming.
• Samsung QN70F supports Motion Xcelerator 144Hz for compatible high-frame-rate content.
• Samsung QN70F supports 4K AI Upscaling for compatible lower-resolution content.
• Samsung QN70F uses Quantum Matrix Technology Slim for picture illumination control.
• Samsung QN70F supports Real Depth Enhancer, Filmmaker Mode and Auto HDR Remastering.
