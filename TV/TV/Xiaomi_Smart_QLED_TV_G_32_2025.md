# Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025

## 1. Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 Overview
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 is a 32-inch QLED HD smart television in Xiaomi India's 2025 G Series.
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 provides 1366 × 768 HD resolution.
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 uses Google TV with Xiaomi PatchWall.

## 2. Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 Display and Picture Quality
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 uses a QLED display with Vivid Picture Engine 2.
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 supports a 60Hz refresh rate.
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 supports HDR10.
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 supports a 90% DCI-P3 colour gamut as specified by Xiaomi.

## 3. Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 Smart TV Features
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 runs Google TV.
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 supports Google Assistant and Google Cast.
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 supports Miracast.
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 supports PatchWall, Kids Mode, Universal Search and smart-TV functions.

## 4. Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 Audio and Sound
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 provides 20W audio output.
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 supports Dolby Audio.
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 supports DTS:X and DTS Virtual:X.

## 5. Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 Connectivity and Ports

• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 includes two USB 2.0 ports.
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 includes Ethernet, AV, headphone and antenna connections.

• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 supports Bluetooth connectivity.

## 6. Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 Gaming Features
• Xiaomi Smart QLED TV G 32 2025 supports Game Mode.
