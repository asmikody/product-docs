# Samsung QN900F




## 1. Samsung QN900F Overview
• Samsung QN900F is a Neo QLED 8K smart television from Samsung's 2025 lineup.
• Samsung QN900F uses model number QA75QN900FUXXL for the 75-inch India-market variant documented here.
• Samsung QN900F is available in 65-inch and 75-inch class sizes in the 2025 series.
• Samsung QN900F runs on Samsung Tizen smart TV software with Samsung Vision AI features.

## 2. Samsung QN900F Display and Picture Quality
• Samsung QN900F uses a Neo QLED display with Quantum Mini LED backlighting.
• Samsung QN900F provides 8K resolution at 7680 × 4320 pixels.
• Samsung QN900F supports Neo Quantum HDR 8K Pro for HDR picture presentation.
• Samsung QN900F supports HDR10+ Adaptive and HDR10+ Gaming.
• Samsung QN900F uses Quantum Matrix Technology Pro for fine control of picture illumination.
• Samsung QN900F supports 8K AI Upscaling for compatible lower-resolution content.

## 3. Samsung QN900F Smart TV Features
• Samsung QN900F runs on Tizen Smart TV and provides access to Samsung TV Plus and supported streaming applications.
• Samsung QN900F supports Bixby voice control and compatible Alexa voice-control features in supported regions.
• Samsung QN900F supports Apple AirPlay, screen mirroring and Samsung SmartThings integration.
• Samsung QN900F includes Samsung Knox security features for supported smart-TV services.
• Samsung QN900F supports multi-device experiences such as mobile-to-TV connection and TV-to-mobile functions.

## 4. Samsung QN900F Audio and Sound
• Samsung QN900F provides a 70W audio output with a 4.2.2-channel speaker configuration on the documented 75-inch variant.
• Samsung QN900F supports Dolby Atmos for compatible immersive audio content.
• Samsung QN900F uses Object Tracking Sound+ to position compatible sound effects around the screen.
• Samsung QN900F supports Q-Symphony for synchronized audio with compatible Samsung soundbars.
• Samsung QN900F provides adaptive sound processing and compatible Bluetooth audio connectivity.

## 5. Samsung QN900F Connectivity and Ports
• Samsung QN900F includes four HDMI ports for external video and audio devices.
• Samsung QN900F supports high-frame-rate HDMI input up to 4K 165Hz on supported ports.
• Samsung QN900F includes two USB-A ports for compatible storage and peripherals.

• Samsung QN900F includes an optical digital audio output and RF broadcast input.
