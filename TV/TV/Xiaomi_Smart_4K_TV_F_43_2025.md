# Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025

## 1. Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 Overview
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 is a 43-inch 4K Fire TV smart television from Xiaomi's 2025 F Series.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 provides 3840 × 2160 4K Ultra HD resolution.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 uses Fire TV built in.

## 2. Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 Display and Picture Quality
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 uses a 4K Ultra HD display with the Vivid Picture Engine 2.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 supports a 60Hz refresh rate.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 supports a 178-degree viewing angle.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 supports Filmmaker Mode.

## 3. Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 Smart TV Features
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 runs Fire TV built in.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 supports Alexa voice assistant through the remote.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 supports Apple AirPlay 2.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 supports more than 12000 applications through the Fire TV Appstore as stated by Xiaomi.

## 4. Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 Audio and Sound
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 provides 24W audio output.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 supports Dolby Audio.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 supports DTS:X and DTS Virtual:X.

## 5. Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 Connectivity and Ports
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 includes three HDMI ports with one eARC port.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 includes two USB 2.0 ports.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 includes Ethernet, AV, headphone and antenna connections.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 supports optical or supported digital audio connectivity.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 supports dual-band Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

## 6. Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 Gaming Features
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 provides Game Mode functionality.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 supports compatible low-latency gaming sources.
• Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 supports 4K gaming content.

## 8. Xiaomi Smart 4K TV F 43 2025 Compatibility and Useful Features
