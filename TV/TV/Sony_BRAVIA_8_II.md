# Sony BRAVIA 8 II

## 1. Sony BRAVIA 8 II Overview

- Sony BRAVIA 8 II is a OLED 4K Google TV.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II uses the model number K-55XR80M2 IN.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II was released for the 2025 model year.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II is documented in Sony India support material.

## 2. Sony BRAVIA 8 II Display and Picture Quality

- Sony BRAVIA 8 II is available with a 55 inches (54.6 inches measured diagonally) display.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II uses OLED display technology.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II supports a 3840 × 2160 pixels resolution.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II supports a 120 Hz panel refresh rate.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II supports HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision for HDR content.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II uses the XR Processor.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II features XR TRILUMINOS Max, XR Contrast Booster 25, XR Clear Image, XR OLED Motion and IMAX Enhanced.

## 3. Sony BRAVIA 8 II Smart TV Features

- Sony BRAVIA 8 II runs on Google TV.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II supports Google TV, Google Play Store, Google Cast, Apple AirPlay and Sony Pictures Core.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II supports Google Assistant voice search with a built-in microphone.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II supports mobile casting and screen mirroring through supported Google Cast or Apple AirPlay features.

## 4. Sony BRAVIA 8 II Audio and Sound

- Sony BRAVIA 8 II uses Acoustic Surface Audio+ with Actuator ×2 and Subwoofer ×2.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II provides 50 W of rated audio output.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II supports Dolby Atmos.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II has the following DTS specification: DTS:X and compatible DTS formats.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II features Voice Zoom 3, 3D Surround Upscaling, Room and User Position Compensation, and Acoustic Centre Sync.

## 5. Sony BRAVIA 8 II Connectivity and Ports

- Sony BRAVIA 8 II includes 4 HDMI inputs.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II supports 4K 120 Hz, eARC, VRR, ALLM and SBTM.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II includes 2 USB ports.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II includes 1 digital audio output.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II includes 1 LAN input.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II supports Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.3.

## 6. Sony BRAVIA 8 II Gaming Features

- Sony BRAVIA 8 II supports VRR on HDMI 3/4, ALLM, 4K 120 Hz input and PlayStation 5 Auto HDR Tone Mapping.
- Sony BRAVIA 8 II supports compatible PCs and game consoles through HDMI.
