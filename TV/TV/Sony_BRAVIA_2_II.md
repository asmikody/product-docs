# Sony BRAVIA 2 II

## 1. Sony BRAVIA 2 II Overview

- Sony BRAVIA 2 II is a Direct LED 4K Google TV.
- Sony BRAVIA 2 II uses the model number K-55S25M2.
- Sony BRAVIA 2 II was released for the 2025 model year.
- Sony BRAVIA 2 II is documented in Sony India support material.

- Sony BRAVIA 2 II is available with a 55 inches (54.6 inches measured diagonally) display.
- Sony BRAVIA 2 II uses Direct LED LCD display technology.
- Sony BRAVIA 2 II supports a 3840 × 2160 pixels resolution.
- Sony BRAVIA 2 II supports a 50 Hz panel refresh rate.
- Sony BRAVIA 2 II supports HDR10 and HLG for HDR content.
- Sony BRAVIA 2 II uses the 4K Processor X1.
- Sony BRAVIA 2 II features Live Colour, Rich Colour Enhancer, Dynamic Contrast Enhancer and 4K X-Reality PRO.
