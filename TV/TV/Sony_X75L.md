# Sony X75L

## 1. Sony X75L Overview

- Sony X75L is a Direct LED 4K Google TV.
- Sony X75L uses the model number KD-55X75L.
- Sony X75L was released for the 2023 model year.
- Sony X75L is documented in Sony India support material.

## 2. Sony X75L Display and Picture Quality

- Sony X75L is available with a 55 inches (54.6 inches measured diagonally) display.
- Sony X75L uses Direct LED LCD display technology.
- Sony X75L supports a 3840 × 2160 pixels resolution.
- Sony X75L supports a 50/60 Hz panel refresh rate.
- Sony X75L supports HDR10 and HLG for HDR content.
- Sony X75L uses the 4K Processor X1.
- Sony X75L features Live Colour, Dynamic Contrast Enhancer, 4K X-Reality PRO and Motionflow XR 200.

## 3. Sony X75L Smart TV Features

- Sony X75L runs on Google TV.
- Sony X75L supports Google TV, Google Play Store, Google Cast, Apple AirPlay and Apple HomeKit.
- Sony X75L supports Google Assistant voice search.
- Sony X75L supports mobile casting and screen mirroring through supported Google Cast or Apple AirPlay features.

## 4. Sony X75L Audio and Sound

- Sony X75L uses Full Range ×2 Open Baffle Speaker.
- Sony X75L provides 20 W of rated audio output.
- Sony X75L Sony does not specify Dolby Atmos for this model.
- Sony X75L has the following DTS specification: DTS not supported.
- Sony X75L features Open Baffle Speaker.

## 5. Sony X75L Connectivity and Ports

- Sony X75L includes 3 HDMI inputs.
- Sony X75L supports eARC and ALLM.
- Sony X75L includes 2 USB ports.
- Sony X75L includes 1 digital audio output.
- Sony X75L includes 1 LAN input.
- Sony X75L supports Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5.0.

## 6. Sony X75L Gaming Features

- Sony X75L supports No VRR, ALLM on HDMI 3 and 4K 60 Hz-class input.
- Sony X75L supports compatible PCs and game consoles through HDMI.
- Sony X75L supports Game picture mode for compatible gaming sources.
- Sony X75L supports PlayStation 5 picture features where specified by Sony.

## 7. Sony X75L Design and Physical Specifications

- Sony X75L has the following physical specification: 1,243 × 729 × 84 mm without stand; 1,243 × 790 × 291 mm with stand; 13.9 kg without stand and 14.5 kg with stand; VESA 400 × 200 mm.

## 8. Sony X75L Compatibility and Useful Features

- Sony X75L is compatible with HDMI-connected media players, PCs and game consoles.
- Sony X75L supports BRAVIA Sync and HDMI-CEC where specified.

## 9. Sony X75L Package and Warranty
