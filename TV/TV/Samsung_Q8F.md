# Samsung Q8F


## 1. Samsung Q8F Overview
• Samsung Q8F is a QLED 4K smart television from Samsung's 2025 lineup.
• Samsung Q8F uses model number QA75Q8FAAUXXL for the 75-inch India-market variant documented here.
• Samsung Q8F is available in 43-inch, 50-inch, 55-inch, 65-inch, 75-inch and 85-inch class sizes in the 2025 lineup.
• Samsung Q8F runs on Samsung Tizen Smart TV software with Samsung Vision AI features.

## 2. Samsung Q8F Display and Picture Quality
• Samsung Q8F uses QLED display technology with a Quantum Dot color layer.
• Samsung Q8F provides 4K resolution at 3840 × 2160 pixels.
• Samsung Q8F supports Quantum HDR and HDR10+.
• Samsung Q8F supports Motion Xcelerator for smoother compatible motion.
• Samsung Q8F supports 4K upscaling for compatible lower-resolution content.
• Samsung Q8F supports 100% Color Volume with Quantum Dot technology as specified by Samsung.
• Samsung Q8F uses Supreme UHD Dimming and Mega Contrast picture processing.

## 3. Samsung Q8F Smart TV Features
• Samsung Q8F runs on Tizen Smart TV and provides access to Samsung TV Plus and supported applications.
• Samsung Q8F supports Bixby voice control and compatible Alexa voice-assistant features in supported regions.
• Samsung Q8F supports Apple AirPlay, screen mirroring, Tap View and multi-device experiences.
• Samsung Q8F supports SmartThings Hub and compatible Matter and smart-home functions.
• Samsung Q8F includes Samsung Knox security features for supported smart-TV services.

## 4. Samsung Q8F Audio and Sound
• Samsung Q8F provides 20W audio output with a 2-channel speaker configuration on the documented 75-inch variant.
• Samsung Q8F supports Object Tracking Sound Lite.
• Samsung Q8F supports Q-Symphony with compatible Samsung soundbars.
• Samsung Q8F supports Active Voice Amplifier and Adaptive Sound+.
• Samsung Q8F supports compatible Bluetooth audio devices.

## 5. Samsung Q8F Connectivity and Ports
• Samsung Q8F includes three HDMI ports.
• Samsung Q8F supports 4K 60Hz high-frame-rate HDMI input on HDMI 1, HDMI 2 and HDMI 3.
• Samsung Q8F includes two USB-A ports on the documented 75-inch configuration.
• Samsung Q8F includes one Ethernet LAN port and one optical digital audio output.
• Samsung Q8F includes RF inputs for compatible terrestrial, cable and satellite broadcasting.
• Samsung Q8F supports Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5.3 on the documented India configuration.

## 6. Samsung Q8F Gaming Features
• Samsung Q8F supports Auto Game Mode through ALLM.
• Samsung Q8F supports Game Motion Plus for compatible game content.
• Samsung Q8F supports Super Ultra Wide Game View and Mini Map Zoom.
• Samsung Q8F supports HGiG and AI Auto Game Mode for supported game content.
• Samsung Q8F provides a 4K 60Hz HDMI input capability on the documented 75-inch variant.

## 7. Samsung Q8F Design and Physical Specifications
• Samsung Q8F uses an AirSlim design intended for a thin wall-mounted or stand-mounted profile.
• Samsung Q8F measures approximately 167.0 × 96.03 × 2.66 cm without the stand on the documented 75-inch variant.
• Samsung Q8F supports a 400 × 400 mm VESA mounting pattern on the documented 75-inch variant.
• Samsung Q8F weighs approximately 29.1 kg without the stand on the documented 75-inch variant.

## 8. Samsung Q8F Compatibility and Useful Features
• Samsung Q8F supports USB media playback and network streaming applications.
• Samsung Q8F supports SmartThings-compatible devices and smart-home control functions.
• Samsung Q8F supports compatible Bluetooth controllers, keyboards and headphones.
• Samsung Q8F supports screen mirroring from compatible mobile devices.
• Samsung Q8F supports compatible soundbars through HDMI eARC or ARC and Q-Symphony.
