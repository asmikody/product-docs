# Sony X82L

## 1. Sony X82L Overview

- Sony X82L is a Direct LED 4K Google TV.
- Sony X82L uses the model number KD-55X82L.
- Sony X82L was released for the 2023 model year.
- Sony X82L is documented in Sony India support material.

## 2. Sony X82L Display and Picture Quality

- Sony X82L is available with a 55 inches (54.6 inches measured diagonally) display.
- Sony X82L uses Direct LED LCD display technology.
- Sony X82L supports a 3840 × 2160 pixels resolution.
- Sony X82L supports a 50/60 Hz panel refresh rate.
- Sony X82L supports HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision for HDR content.
- Sony X82L uses the 4K HDR Processor X1.
- Sony X82L features TRILUMINOS Pro, Object-based HDR remaster, 4K X-Reality PRO and Motionflow XR 200.

## 3. Sony X82L Smart TV Features

- Sony X82L runs on Google TV.
- Sony X82L supports Google TV, Google Play Store, Google Cast, Apple AirPlay and Apple HomeKit.
- Sony X82L supports Google Assistant voice search with a built-in microphone.
- Sony X82L supports mobile casting and screen mirroring through supported Google Cast or Apple AirPlay features.

## 4. Sony X82L Audio and Sound

- Sony X82L uses Acoustic Multi-Audio with Full Range Bass Reflex ×2 and Tweeter ×2.
- Sony X82L provides 20 W of rated audio output.
- Sony X82L supports Dolby Atmos.
- Sony X82L has the following DTS specification: DTS Digital Surround.
- Sony X82L features Sound Positioning Tweeter, X-Balanced Speaker and Room Compensation.

## 5. Sony X82L Connectivity and Ports

- Sony X82L includes 4 HDMI inputs.
- Sony X82L supports eARC and ALLM.
- Sony X82L includes 2 USB ports.
- Sony X82L includes 1 digital audio output.
- Sony X82L includes 1 LAN input.
- Sony X82L supports Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 4.2.

## 6. Sony X82L Gaming Features

- Sony X82L supports No VRR, ALLM on HDMI 3/4 and 4K 60 Hz-class input.
- Sony X82L supports compatible PCs and game consoles through HDMI.
- Sony X82L supports Game picture mode for compatible gaming sources.
- Sony X82L supports PlayStation 5 picture features where specified by Sony.

## 7. Sony X82L Design and Physical Specifications

- Sony X82L has the following physical specification: 1,233 × 713 × 72 mm without stand; 1,233 × 785 × 336 mm with stand; 15.9 kg without stand and 17 kg with stand; VESA 300 × 300 mm.
- Sony X82L supports wall mounting through its specified VESA pattern where one is published.
- Sony X82L uses a Sony-specified table-top stand configuration.

## 8. Sony X82L Compatibility and Useful Features

- Sony X82L is compatible with HDMI-connected media players, PCs and game consoles.
- Sony X82L supports BRAVIA Sync and HDMI-CEC where specified.
- Sony X82L supports Sony accessibility features provided by its software.

## 9. Sony X82L Package and Warranty

- Sony X82L includes Table Top Stand, Voice Remote Control, batteries, AC power cord and setup documentation.
- Sony X82L is covered by the applicable Sony India warranty terms for the specific purchase and region.
