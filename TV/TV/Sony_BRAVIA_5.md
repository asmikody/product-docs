# Sony BRAVIA 5

## 1. Sony BRAVIA 5 Overview

- Sony BRAVIA 5 is a Mini LED 4K Google TV.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 uses the model number K-55XR55A.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 was released for the 2025 model year.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 is documented in Sony India support material.

## 2. Sony BRAVIA 5 Display and Picture Quality

- Sony BRAVIA 5 is available with a 55 inches (54.6 inches measured diagonally) display.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 uses Mini LED LCD display technology.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports a 3840 × 2160 pixels resolution.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports a 120 Hz panel refresh rate.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision for HDR content.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 uses the XR Processor.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 features XR Backlight Master Drive, XR Contrast Booster 10, XR TRILUMINOS Pro, XR Clear Image and XR Motion Clarity.

## 3. Sony BRAVIA 5 Smart TV Features

- Sony BRAVIA 5 runs on Google TV.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports Google TV, Google Play Store, Google Cast, Apple AirPlay and Sony Pictures Core.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports Google Assistant voice search with a built-in microphone.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports mobile casting and screen mirroring through supported Google Cast or Apple AirPlay features.

## 4. Sony BRAVIA 5 Audio and Sound

- Sony BRAVIA 5 uses Acoustic Multi-Audio with Sound Positioning Tweeter and X-Balanced Speaker.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 provides 40 W of rated audio output.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports Dolby Atmos.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 has the following DTS specification: DTS:X and compatible DTS formats.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 features Voice Zoom 3, 3D Surround Upscaling, Room and User Position Compensation, and Acoustic Centre Sync.

## 5. Sony BRAVIA 5 Connectivity and Ports

- Sony BRAVIA 5 includes 4 HDMI inputs.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports 4K 120 Hz, eARC, VRR, ALLM and SBTM.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 includes 2 USB ports.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 includes 1 digital audio output.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 includes 1 LAN input.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.3.

## 6. Sony BRAVIA 5 Gaming Features

- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports VRR, ALLM, 4K 120 Hz input and PlayStation 5 picture features.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports compatible PCs and game consoles through HDMI.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports Game picture mode for compatible gaming sources.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports PlayStation 5 picture features where specified by Sony.

## 7. Sony BRAVIA 5 Design and Physical Specifications

- Sony BRAVIA 5 has the following physical specification: 1,228 × 709 × 57 mm without stand; Sony documents the remaining stand dimensions and weight for this model.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports wall mounting through its specified VESA pattern where one is published.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 uses a Sony-specified table-top stand configuration.

## 8. Sony BRAVIA 5 Compatibility and Useful Features

- Sony BRAVIA 5 is compatible with HDMI-connected media players, PCs and game consoles.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports BRAVIA Sync and HDMI-CEC where specified.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 supports Sony accessibility features provided by its software.

## 9. Sony BRAVIA 5 Package and Warranty

- Sony BRAVIA 5 includes Voice Remote Control, batteries, AC power cord and setup documentation.
- Sony BRAVIA 5 is covered by the applicable Sony India warranty terms for the specific purchase and region.
