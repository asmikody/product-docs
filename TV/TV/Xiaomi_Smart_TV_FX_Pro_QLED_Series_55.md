# Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55

## 1. Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 Overview
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 is a 55-inch QLED 4K smart television from Xiaomi India's 2025 FX Pro QLED series.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 provides 3840 × 2160 4K Ultra HD resolution.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 uses Fire TV built in as the smart-TV platform.

## 2. Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 Display and Picture Quality
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 uses a QLED display with the Vivid Picture Engine 2.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports a 60Hz refresh rate and DLG 120Hz on compatible content.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports HDR10+ and compatible HDR content.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports Filmmaker Mode.

## 3. Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 Smart TV Features
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 runs Fire TV built in.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports Alexa voice assistant through the bundled remote.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports Apple AirPlay 2 and Miracast.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports supported streaming applications through the Fire TV Appstore.

## 4. Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 Audio and Sound
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 provides 34W audio output.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports Dolby Audio.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports DTS:X and DTS Virtual:X.

## 5. Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 Connectivity and Ports
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 includes three HDMI ports with one eARC port.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 includes two USB 2.0 ports.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 includes one Ethernet port, one AV input and one headphone output.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 includes antenna connectivity and optical digital audio output.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports dual-band Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

## 6. Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 Gaming Features
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports ALLM for compatible low-latency gaming.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports compatible HDMI-connected gaming consoles and PCs.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports Game Mode and motion processing on supported content.

## 7. Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 Design and Physical Specifications
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 uses a metal bezel-less design.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 measures approximately 1226 × 76 × 711 mm without the stand.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 weighs approximately 9.1 kg without the base.

## 8. Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 Compatibility and Useful Features
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports AirPlay 2 and Miracast-compatible devices.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports USB media playback.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports compatible Bluetooth headphones, speakers and controllers.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 supports external soundbars through HDMI eARC or optical output.

## 9. Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 Package and Warranty
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 is supplied with the TV, remote, manual, stand units, screws, batteries and power cord.
• Xiaomi Smart TV FX Pro QLED Series 55 includes a 1-year comprehensive warranty with an additional 1-year extension on the 55-inch model as listed by Xiaomi India.
