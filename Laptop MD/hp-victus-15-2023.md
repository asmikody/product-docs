# HP Victus 15 (2023) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP Victus 15 (2023) Overview and Design
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) officially launched in April 2023 with strong entry-level gaming market presence through 2024.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) belongs to the 15.6-inch budget entry gaming and student laptop class.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) chassis is constructed from post-consumer recycled Mica Silver polymers with embossed V-logo branding.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) carries an official device weight of approximately 2.29 kg.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) measures a portable gaming chassis profile thickness of 23.5 mm.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) targets high school gamers, budget PC players, esports casuals, and undergraduate students.

## 2. HP Victus 15 (2023) Configurations
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) base config equips Core i5-13420H, RTX 2050 4 GB, 8 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, FHD 144Hz.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 6 GB discrete GPU and 16 GB RAM.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) performance config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 6 GB discrete GPU and 1 TB SSD.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) flagship config adds an Intel Core i7-13700H processor and an RTX 4050 discrete GPU.

## 3. HP Victus 15 (2023) CPU Specifications
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-H mobile microarchitecture.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) CPU utilizes the Intel 13th Gen Core H performance mobile processor series.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-13700H.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i5-13420H.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) Core i5-13420H features 8 physical cores and 12 concurrent threads.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) CPU core layout comprises 4 Raptor Cove P-cores and 4 Gracemont E-cores.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) Core i5-13420H P-cores operate at a 2.1 GHz base and up to 4.6 GHz boost.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) Core i5-13420H E-cores operate at a 1.5 GHz base and up to 3.4 GHz boost.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR4-3200 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) CPU integrates Intel UHD Graphics with 48 execution units operating at 1.40 GHz.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 NVMe storage.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) CPU integrates high-speed USB host controllers for external peripherals.

## 4. HP Victus 15 (2023) GPU Specifications
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace or Ampere GPU microarchitecture.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) RTX 4050 GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) flagship features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 Laptop GPU with 2,560 CUDA cores.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) mid-tier features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 6 GB Laptop GPU with 2,560 CUDA cores.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) base features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2050 4 GB Laptop GPU with 2,048 CUDA cores.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) RTX 4050 config equips 6 GB of dedicated GDDR6 VRAM across a 96-bit memory bus.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) RTX 4050 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 192 GB/s at 16 Gbps effective speed.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) discrete GPU operates at a configured 60 W base TGP plus 15 W Dynamic Boost.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 75 W TGP.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) utilizes standard NVIDIA Optimus automatic graphics switching technology.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 compute architecture, while ROCm is unsupported on NVIDIA hardware.

## 5. HP Victus 15 (2023) AI and Performance
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) does not incorporate a dedicated silicon NPU on its Intel 13th Gen processor.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) RTX 4050 discrete GPU delivers up to 194 INT8 TOPS of dedicated AI compute performance.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) RTX 4050 discrete GPU achieves up to 9.2 TFLOPS of FP32 compute performance.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) does not qualify for Microsoft Copilot+ PC certification due to lacking a 40 TOPS NPU.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) discrete GPU incorporates an 8th-generation NVENC hardware video encoder with AV1 support.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) discrete GPU includes a 5th-generation NVDEC engine supporting 8K 60 fps AV1 and HEVC decoding.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) Core i5-13420H achieves approximately 11,200 points in Cinebench R23 multi-core benchmark testing.

## 6. HP Victus 15 (2023) Power, Thermals and Cooling
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) CPU is configured with a 45 W sustained PL1 and 95 W peak PL2 turbo limit.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) sustains a maximum continuous combined gaming power dissipation of 105 W in Performance mode.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) cooling system incorporates dual high-output cooling fans with twin thick copper heatpipes.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) applies high-grade thermal grease interface material across both processor packages.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) provides Quiet, Default, and Performance thermal profiles via OMEN Gaming Hub software.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) integrates a 3-cell 52.5 Wh or optional 4-cell 70 Wh lithium-ion rechargeable battery pack.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) ships with a standard 150 W or 200 W barrel AC power adapter.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) supports HP Fast Charge technology, reaching 50 percent charge in 30 minutes.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) triggers thermal safety throttling when GPU temperatures hit 87 °C or CPU exceeds 95 °C.

- The HP Victus 15 (2023) FHD panel delivers 250 nits brightness, 45% NTSC color gamut, and 9 ms response time.
- The HP Victus 15 (2023) keyboard includes a dedicated numeric keypad with integrated white LED backlighting.
