# ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) Buyer's Guide

- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) belongs to the 16-inch ultra-thin next-generation AI PC laptop class.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) chassis is crafted from Ceraluminum, an innovative ceramicized aluminum alloy.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) carries a remarkably light device mass of approximately 1.50 kg.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) measures an ultra-slim profile thickness between 11.9 mm and 12.9 mm.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) targets data analysts, software engineers, digital creators, and business leaders seeking large portable screens.

- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) CPU belongs to the AMD Ryzen AI 300 performance mobile APU series.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the AMD Ryzen AI 9 HX 370.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) base processor configuration is the AMD Ryzen AI 9 365.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) Ryzen AI 9 HX 370 features 12 physical cores and 24 concurrent threads.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) core layout comprises 4 high-performance Zen 5 cores and 8 compact Zen 5c cores.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) Ryzen AI 9 HX 370 operates at a 2.0 GHz base clock speed.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) Ryzen AI 9 HX 370 achieves a maximum boost clock speed of up to 5.1 GHz.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared L3 cache memory.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5X-7500 MT/s memory.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) CPU integrates AMD Radeon 890M graphics based on RDNA 3.5 architecture.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) integrated Radeon 890M GPU features 16 compute units operating at up to 2.9 GHz.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, AMD64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and native AVX-512.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for internal NVMe storage connectivity.
- The ASUS Zenbook S 16 (2024) CPU integrates dual native USB4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.
