# Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) Overview and Design
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) officially launched in January 2024 at CES 2024.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) belongs to the entry-to-midrange 15.6-inch budget gaming laptop class.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) chassis is constructed from Luna Grey polycarbonate with rear cooling exhaust ports.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 2.38 kg.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) measures an accessible gaming chassis profile thickness of 23.9 mm.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) targets budget-conscious gamers, STEM students, esports beginners, and casual creators.

## 2. Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) Configurations
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) base config equips Core i5-13450HX, RTX 3050 6 GB, 8 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, FHD 144Hz.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 6 GB discrete GPU and 16 GB RAM.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) performance config adds an Intel Core i7-13650HX processor and an RTX 4060 8 GB discrete GPU.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) flagship config adds an Intel Core i7-14700HX processor, 32 GB RAM, and a 1 TB SSD.

## 3. Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-HX microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel 13th and 14th Gen Core HX mobile processor series.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-14700HX.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-13650HX.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) Core i7-13650HX features 14 physical cores and 20 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) CPU core layout comprises 6 Raptor Cove P-cores and 8 Gracemont E-cores.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) Core i7-13650HX P-cores operate at a 2.6 GHz base and up to 4.9 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) Core i7-13650HX E-cores operate at a 1.9 GHz base and up to 3.6 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-4800 MT/s or DDR5-5600 MT/s memory.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) CPU integrates Intel UHD Graphics with 16 execution units operating at 1.55 GHz.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) CPU integrates high-speed USB host controllers for peripheral connectivity.

## 4. Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace GPU microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) flagship features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU with 3,072 CUDA cores.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) mid-tier features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 Laptop GPU with 2,560 CUDA cores.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) base features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 6 GB Laptop GPU with 2,560 CUDA cores.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) RTX 4060 GPU incorporates 96 4th-gen Tensor cores and 24 3rd-gen RT cores.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) RTX 4060 config equips 8 GB of GDDR6 VRAM across a 128-bit memory bus.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) RTX 4060 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 256 GB/s at 16 Gbps effective speed.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) RTX 4060 achieves a factory boost GPU clock speed of up to 2370 MHz.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 100 W base TGP plus 15 W Dynamic Boost.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 115 W TGP.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) integrates Advanced Optimus automatic graphics switching with dedicated hardware MUX switch.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The Lenovo LOQ 15IRX9 (2024) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 compute architecture, while ROCm is unsupported on NVIDIA hardware.
