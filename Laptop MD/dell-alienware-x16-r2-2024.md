# Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) Overview and Design
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) officially launched in January 2024 at CES 2024.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) belongs to the 16-inch ultra-thin flagship luxury gaming laptop class.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) chassis is constructed from Lunar Silver anodized aluminum with micro-RGB rear stadium lighting.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 2.72 kg.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) measures an ultra-thin gaming chassis profile thickness of only 18.57 mm.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) targets luxury enthusiasts, extreme gamers, creative professionals, and tech collectors.

## 2. Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) Configurations
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) base config equips Core Ultra 7 155H, RTX 4070, 16 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD, QHD+ 240Hz.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) mid-tier config adds an Intel Core Ultra 9 185H processor and 32 GB RAM.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) performance config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 12 GB discrete GPU.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) flagship config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 16 GB discrete GPU and 4 TB SSD.

## 3. Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake-H microarchitecture.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) CPU belongs to the Intel Core Ultra 100H performance mobile series.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 9 185H.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155H.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) Core Ultra 9 185H features 16 physical cores and 22 concurrent threads.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) core layout comprises 6 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) Core Ultra 9 185H P-cores operate at a 2.3 GHz base and up to 5.1 GHz boost.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) Core Ultra 9 185H E-cores operate at a 1.8 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5X-7467 MT/s memory.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics featuring 8 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) integrated Intel Arc GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 2.35 GHz.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) CPU integrates a native Thunderbolt 4 host controller providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace GPU microarchitecture.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) flagship features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 Laptop GPU with 9,728 CUDA cores.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) tier-two features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 Laptop GPU with 7,424 CUDA cores.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) base features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop GPU with 4,608 CUDA cores.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) RTX 4090 GPU incorporates 304 4th-gen Tensor cores and 76 3rd-gen RT cores.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) RTX 4090 config equips 16 GB of GDDR6 VRAM across a 256-bit memory bus.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) RTX 4080 config equips 12 GB of GDDR6 VRAM across a 192-bit memory bus.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) RTX 4090 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 576 GB/s at 18 Gbps effective speed.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) RTX 4090 achieves a factory boost GPU clock speed of up to 2040 MHz.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 150 W base TGP plus 25 W Dynamic Boost.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 175 W TGP.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) integrates NVIDIA Advanced Optimus automatic graphics switching with dedicated hardware MUX switch.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 compute architecture, while ROCm is unsupported on NVIDIA hardware.

- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) integrates a dedicated Intel AI Boost NPU delivering up to 11 TOPS neural compute.

- The Dell Alienware x16 R2 (2024) Core Ultra 9 185H achieves approximately 17,900 points in Cinebench R23 multi-core benchmark testing.
