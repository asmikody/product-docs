# Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) Overview and Design
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) officially launched in March 2024 as Dell essential commercial workforce notebook.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) belongs to the 14-inch essential commercial fleet and educational laptop class.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) chassis is crafted from durable post-consumer recycled plastics meeting MIL-STD-810H durability standards.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.50 kg.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) measures a functional commercial chassis profile thickness of 18.6 mm.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) targets call center agents, enterprise back-office staff, public sector workers, and students.

## 2. Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) Configurations
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) base config equips Core 3 100U, Intel Graphics, 8 GB RAM, 256 GB SSD, FHD IPS.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) mid-tier config adds an Intel Core Ultra 5 125U vPro processor and 16 GB DDR5 RAM.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) performance config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 155U vPro processor and 512 GB SSD.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) flagship config adds dual SO-DIMM 32 GB DDR5 RAM, a 1 TB SSD, and an FHD touch display.

## 3. Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake-U or Raptor Lake-U Refresh microarchitectures.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) CPU belongs to the Intel Core Ultra 100U vPro or Core 100U commercial series.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155U with vPro Enterprise.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 5 125U with vPro Essentials.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) Core Ultra 5 125U features 12 physical cores and 14 concurrent threads.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) core layout comprises 2 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) Core Ultra 5 125U P-cores operate at a 1.3 GHz base and up to 4.3 GHz boost.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) Core Ultra 5 125U E-cores operate at a 0.8 GHz base and up to 3.6 GHz boost.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Graphics with 4 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) integrated Intel GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 1.85 GHz.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2230 NVMe storage.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) CPU integrates USB 3.2 Gen 2x2 Type-C controllers providing 20 Gbps bandwidth.

## 4. Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) graphics system utilizes the integrated Intel Graphics 4 Xe-core processor.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) GPU tile is fabricated on TSMC 5 nm N5 process technology.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) Intel graphics silicon integrates 64 vector engines and 4 hardware ray tracing units.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) GPU dynamically shares up to 16 GB of system DDR5 memory as VRAM.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) memory bus delivers up to 89.6 GB/s bandwidth to the integrated graphics core.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) integrated graphics processor operates at a peak boost frequency of 1.85 GHz.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) integrated GPU operates within a shared 15 W nominal processor power envelope.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) drives dual external monitors over USB-C DisplayPort and full-size HDMI 1.4.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The Dell Latitude 3450 (2024) GPU supports Intel oneAPI Level Zero compute architecture, while CUDA is unsupported.
