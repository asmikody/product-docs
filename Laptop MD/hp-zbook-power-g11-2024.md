# HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) Overview and Design
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) officially launched in April 2024 as an accessible enterprise mobile workstation.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) belongs to the 16-inch performance entry mobile workstation engineering laptop class.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) chassis is crafted from Space Silver all-aluminum metal meeting MIL-STD-810H durability standards.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 2.04 kg.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) measures a functional workstation chassis profile thickness of 22.9 mm.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) targets CAD drafters, architecture students, 3D product designers, and engineering consultants.

## 2. HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) Configurations
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) base config equips Core Ultra 7 155H, RTX 500 Ada, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, FHD+.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA RTX 1000 Ada 6 GB discrete GPU and 32 GB RAM.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) performance config adds an NVIDIA RTX 2000 Ada 8 GB discrete GPU and 1 TB SSD.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) flagship config adds RTX 3000 Ada 8 GB GPU, Core Ultra 9 185H, and 64 GB RAM.

## 3. HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake-H microarchitecture.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) CPU belongs to the Intel Core Ultra 100H vPro enterprise mobile processor series.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 9 185H with vPro Enterprise.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155H with vPro Enterprise.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H features 16 physical cores and 22 concurrent threads.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) core layout comprises 6 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H P-cores operate at a 1.4 GHz base and up to 4.8 GHz boost.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H E-cores operate at a 0.9 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics featuring 8 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) integrated Intel Arc GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 2.25 GHz.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) CPU integrates a native Thunderbolt 4 host controller providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace professional workstation microarchitecture.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) flagship features an NVIDIA RTX 3000 Ada Generation Laptop GPU with 4,608 CUDA cores.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) tier-two features an NVIDIA RTX 2000 Ada Generation Laptop GPU with 3,072 CUDA cores.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) tier-three features an NVIDIA RTX 1000 Ada Generation Laptop GPU with 2,560 CUDA cores.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) base features an NVIDIA RTX 500 Ada Generation Laptop GPU with 2,048 CUDA cores.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) RTX 3000 Ada config equips 8 GB of GDDR6 VRAM across a 128-bit memory bus.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) RTX 3000 Ada delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 256 GB/s at 16 Gbps effective speed.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 50 W base TGP plus 10 W Dynamic Boost.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 60 W TGP.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) delivers Independent Software Vendor (ISV) certifications for AutoCAD, Revit, SolidWorks, and Bentley.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP ZBook Power G11 (2024) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 and enterprise OptiX ray tracing compute architectures.
