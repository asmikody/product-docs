- The Dell Precision 5690 (2024) Core Ultra 9 185H features 16 physical cores and 22 concurrent threads.
- The Dell Precision 5690 (2024) core layout comprises 6 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The Dell Precision 5690 (2024) Core Ultra 9 185H P-cores operate at a 2.3 GHz base and up to 5.1 GHz boost.
- The Dell Precision 5690 (2024) Core Ultra 9 185H E-cores operate at a 1.8 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The Dell Precision 5690 (2024) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Dell Precision 5690 (2024) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5X-7467 MT/s memory.
- The Dell Precision 5690 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics featuring 8 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The Dell Precision 5690 (2024) integrated Intel Arc GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 2.35 GHz.
- The Dell Precision 5690 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The Dell Precision 5690 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for dual enterprise M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The Dell Precision 5690 (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.
