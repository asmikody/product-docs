- The Dell G16 (7630) (2023) CPU core layout comprises 8 Raptor Cove P-cores and 16 Gracemont E-cores.

- The Dell G16 (7630) (2023) Core i9-13900HX E-cores operate at a 1.6 GHz base and up to 3.9 GHz boost.
- The Dell G16 (7630) (2023) processor incorporates a massive 36 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.

- The Dell G16 (7630) (2023) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
