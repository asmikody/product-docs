# Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 3. Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV technology.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel Core Ultra 100U and 100H mobile processor series.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155U.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U features 12 physical cores and 14 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) core layout comprises 2 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U P-cores operate at 1.7 GHz base and 4.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U E-cores operate at 1.2 GHz base and 3.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5X-6400 MT/s memory.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Graphics featuring 4 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) integrated Intel GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 1.95 GHz.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 NVMe storage drives.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Yoga Gen 9 (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.
