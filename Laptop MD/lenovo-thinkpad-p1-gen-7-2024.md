# Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) Overview and Design
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) officially launched in April 2024.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) belongs to the 16-inch premium mobile AI workstation laptop class.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) chassis is constructed from CNC-machined aluminum with carbon fiber reinforcement.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.82 kg.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) measures a slim workstation profile thickness of only 17.05 mm.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) targets CAD engineers, data scientists, architects, and visual effects artists.

## 2. Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) Configurations
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) base config equips Core Ultra 7 155H, Intel Arc, 32 GB LPCAMM2, 1 TB SSD, QHD+ IPS.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA RTX 1000 Ada Generation 6 GB mobile workstation GPU.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) professional config adds an NVIDIA RTX 3000 Ada Generation 8 GB GPU and 64 GB RAM.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) flagship config adds an RTX 4070 Laptop GPU, Core Ultra 9 185H, and 4K OLED touch.

## 3. Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake-H microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) CPU belongs to the Intel Core Ultra 100H performance mobile series.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 9 185H.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155H.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H features 16 physical cores and 22 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) core layout comprises 6 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H P-cores operate at a 1.4 GHz base and up to 4.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H E-cores operate at a 0.9 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) adopts the groundbreaking LPCAMM2 LPDDR5X-7467 MT/s modular replaceable memory standard.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics featuring 8 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) integrated Intel Arc GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 2.25 GHz.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 and PCIe 5.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace architecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) flagship features an NVIDIA RTX 3000 Ada Generation or RTX 4070 Laptop GPU.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) RTX 3000 Ada features 4,608 CUDA cores and 144 4th-gen Tensor cores.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) RTX 3000 Ada equips 8 GB of GDDR6 ECC VRAM across a 128-bit memory bus.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) discrete GPU delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 256 GB/s at 16 Gbps effective speed.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 60 W base TGP plus 20 W Dynamic Boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 80 W TGP.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) integrates a hardware MUX switch accompanied by NVIDIA Advanced Optimus automatic display switching.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) GPU supports ISV certified workstation drivers, DirectX 12 Ultimate, and Vulkan 1.3.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 compute architecture, while ROCm is unsupported on NVIDIA hardware.
