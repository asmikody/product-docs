# Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) Buyer's Guide

- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) belongs to the 16-inch ultra-premium forged carbon luxury flagship gaming laptop class.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) chassis features a unique forged carbon A-cover where every individual unit has a distinct pattern.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 2.56 kg.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) measures a flagship chassis profile thickness of 22.7 mm.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) targets luxury enthusiasts, extreme gamers, 3D artists, and mobile workstation power users.

## 2. Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) Configurations
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) 16IRX9 base config equips Core i9-14900HX, RTX 4080, 32 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD, 3.2K Mini-LED.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) 16IRX9 tier-two config adds a 2 TB dual-SSD option with RTX 4080 and 32 GB RAM.

## 3. Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake Refresh-HX microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel 14th Gen Core HX enthusiast mobile processor series.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i9-14900HX.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) Core i9-14900HX features 24 physical cores and 32 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) CPU core layout comprises 8 Raptor Cove P-cores and 16 Gracemont E-cores.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) Core i9-14900HX P-cores operate at a 2.2 GHz base and up to 5.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) Core i9-14900HX E-cores operate at a 1.6 GHz base and up to 4.1 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) processor incorporates a massive 36 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s or DDR5-6400 MT/s memory.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) CPU integrates Intel UHD Graphics with 32 execution units operating at 1.65 GHz.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace GPU microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.

- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) RTX 4090 config equips 16 GB of GDDR6 VRAM across a 256-bit memory bus.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) RTX 4080 config equips 12 GB of GDDR6 VRAM across a 192-bit memory bus.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) RTX 4090 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 576 GB/s at 18 Gbps effective speed.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) RTX 4090 achieves a factory boost GPU clock speed of up to 2040 MHz.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 150 W base TGP plus 25 W Dynamic Boost.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 175 W TGP.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) integrates Advanced Optimus automatic graphics switching with dedicated hardware MUX switch.
- The Lenovo Legion 9i Gen 9 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
