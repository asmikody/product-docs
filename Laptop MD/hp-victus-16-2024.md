# HP Victus 16 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP Victus 16 (2024) Overview and Design
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) officially launched in January 2024 at CES 2024.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) belongs to the 16.1-inch accessible mainstream budget gaming laptop class.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) chassis is crafted from Mica Silver or Ceramic White durable post-consumer recycled polymers.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 2.33 kg.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) measures a practical gaming profile thickness of 23.9 mm.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) targets college students, entry-level esports gamers, casual creators, and budget-conscious buyers.

## 2. HP Victus 16 (2024) Configurations
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) base config equips Core i5-14500HX, RTX 4050, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, FHD 144Hz.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 8 GB discrete GPU and 1 TB SSD.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) performance config adds an Intel Core i7-14700HX processor and an RTX 4070 8 GB discrete GPU.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) flagship config adds 32 GB DDR5 RAM, a 1 TB SSD, and a QHD 240Hz display.

## 3. HP Victus 16 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake Refresh-HX microarchitecture.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel 14th Gen Core HX enthusiast mobile processor series.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-14700HX.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i5-14500HX.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) Core i7-14700HX features 20 physical cores and 28 concurrent threads.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) CPU core layout comprises 8 Raptor Cove P-cores and 12 Gracemont E-cores.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) Core i7-14700HX P-cores operate at a 2.1 GHz base and up to 5.5 GHz boost.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) Core i7-14700HX E-cores operate at a 1.5 GHz base and up to 3.9 GHz boost.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) processor incorporates a massive 33 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) CPU integrates Intel UHD Graphics with 32 execution units operating at 1.60 GHz.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 NVMe storage.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) CPU integrates high-speed USB host controllers for gaming peripherals.

## 4. HP Victus 16 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace GPU microarchitecture.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) flagship features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop GPU with 4,608 CUDA cores.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) mid-tier features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU with 3,072 CUDA cores.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) base features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 Laptop GPU with 2,560 CUDA cores.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) RTX 4070 and RTX 4060 configs equip 8 GB of GDDR6 VRAM on 128-bit buses.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) RTX 4070 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 256 GB/s at 16 Gbps effective speed.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 100 W base TGP plus 20 W Dynamic Boost.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 120 W TGP.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) integrates a dedicated hardware MUX switch accompanied by NVIDIA Advanced Optimus switching.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 compute architecture, while ROCm is unsupported on NVIDIA hardware.

## 5. HP Victus 16 (2024) AI and Performance
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) does not incorporate a dedicated silicon NPU on its Intel 14th Gen processor.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) RTX 4070 discrete GPU delivers up to 321 INT8 TOPS of dedicated AI compute performance.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) integrates the OMEN Gaming Hub AI optimizer for automated game performance profile tuning.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) does not qualify for Microsoft Copilot+ PC certification due to lacking a 40 TOPS NPU.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) discrete GPU incorporates an 8th-generation NVENC hardware video encoder with AV1 support.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) discrete GPU includes a 5th-generation NVDEC engine supporting 8K 60 fps AV1 and HEVC decoding.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) Core i7-14700HX achieves approximately 24,500 points in Cinebench R23 multi-core benchmark testing.

## 6. HP Victus 16 (2024) Power, Thermals and Cooling
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) CPU is configured with a 55 W sustained PL1 and 115 W peak PL2 turbo limit.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) sustains a maximum continuous combined gaming power dissipation of 150 W in Performance mode.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) cooling system incorporates dual high-airflow cooling fans and four wide copper heatpipes.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) applies high-grade thermal grease interface material across both processor dies.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) provides Quiet, Default, and Performance thermal profiles via OMEN Gaming Hub software.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) integrates a 4-cell 70 Wh or optional 6-cell 83 Wh lithium-ion rechargeable battery pack.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) ships with a standard 200 W or 230 W barrel AC power adapter.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) supports HP Fast Charge technology, reaching 50 percent charge in 30 minutes.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) triggers thermal safety throttling when GPU temperatures hit 87 °C or CPU exceeds 95 °C.

## 7. HP Victus 16 (2024) Display, Ports and Connectivity
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) features a 16.1-inch FHD 1920 x 1080 pixel 16:9 144 Hz anti-glare IPS display.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) FHD panel delivers 300 nits brightness, 100% sRGB color gamut coverage, and low blue light.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) keyboard includes a dedicated numeric keypad and single-zone RGB or white backlighting.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) includes one USB Type-C 5 Gbps port supporting DisplayPort 1.4 and Power Delivery.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) features three USB Type-A 5 Gbps ports and a full-size HDMI 2.1 port.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) includes a dedicated 1 Gbps RJ-45 Ethernet jack and a 3.5 mm combination audio jack.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) features an FHD 1080p camera with integrated dual microphones and a privacy shutter.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) provides wireless networking via Wi-Fi 6 dual-band 802.11ax and Bluetooth 5.3.

## 8. HP Victus 16 (2024) Compatibility and Constraints
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) requires UEFI BIOS version F.04 or newer for full hardware feature enablement.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) requires minimum display drivers of NVIDIA Game Ready Driver 551.23 or newer.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) officially supports 64-bit Microsoft Windows 11 Home and Windows 11 Pro operating systems.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) memory is fully upgradeable via two internal DDR5 SO-DIMM slots supporting up to 32 GB.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) internal storage is user-upgradeable via a single internal M.2 2280 PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD slot.
- The HP Victus 16 (2024) includes a standard 1-year HP limited consumer warranty with eligible Care Pack extensions.
