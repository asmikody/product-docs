# Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) Overview and Design
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) officially launched in April 2024.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) belongs to the enterprise backbone 14-inch modular productivity laptop class.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) chassis is co-engineered with iFixit featuring easily repairable modular internal architecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.38 kg.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) measures an enterprise profile thickness of 17.7 mm.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) targets corporate IT departments, software developers, finance teams, and fleet enterprise deployments.

## 2. Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) Configurations
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) base config equips Core Ultra 5 125U, 16 GB RAM, 256 GB SSD, WUXGA IPS.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) AMD config adds an AMD Ryzen 7 PRO 8840U processor with Radeon 780M graphics.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) performance config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 155H processor with Intel Arc graphics.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) flagship config adds dual SO-DIMM 64 GB RAM, a 2 TB SSD, and a 2.8K OLED display.

## 3. Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake-U and Meteor Lake-H microarchitectures.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel Core Ultra 100U and 100H mobile processor series.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) primary Intel processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155U.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U features 12 physical cores and 14 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) core layout comprises 2 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U P-cores operate at a 1.7 GHz base and up to 4.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U E-cores operate at a 1.2 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Graphics featuring 4 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) integrated Intel GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 1.95 GHz.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) graphics system utilizes the integrated Intel Graphics 4 Xe-core processor.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) GPU tile is fabricated on TSMC 5 nm N5 process technology.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) Intel graphics silicon integrates 64 vector engines and 4 hardware ray tracing units.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) GPU dynamically shares up to 32 GB of system DDR5 memory as VRAM.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) memory bus delivers up to 89.6 GB/s bandwidth across dual DDR5 SO-DIMM channels.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) integrated graphics processor operates at a peak boost frequency of 1.95 GHz.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) integrated GPU operates within a shared 15 W nominal processor power envelope.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) delivers reliable graphics acceleration for enterprise multi-display docking setups.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 5 (2024) GPU supports Intel oneAPI Level Zero compute architecture, while CUDA is unsupported.
