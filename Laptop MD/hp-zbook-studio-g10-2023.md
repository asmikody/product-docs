# HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) Overview and Design
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) officially launched in May 2023 as HP flagship thin-and-light mobile workstation.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) belongs to the 16-inch ultra-premium mobile workstation creator laptop class.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) chassis is crafted from Space Silver CNC-machined recycled aluminum and magnesium.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.73 kg.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) measures a remarkably slim workstation chassis profile thickness of 19.0 mm.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) targets visual effects creators, 3D animators, game developers, and architectural visualizers.

## 2. HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) Configurations
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) base config equips Core i7-13700H, RTX 2000 Ada, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, WQXGA 120Hz.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA RTX 3000 Ada 8 GB discrete GPU and 32 GB RAM.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) gaming-class config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 12 GB GPU and 1 TB SSD.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) flagship config adds an NVIDIA RTX 4000 Ada 12 GB GPU, Core i9-13900H, 64 GB RAM, 4K OLED.

## 3. HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) CPU Specifications
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-H mobile microarchitecture.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) CPU utilizes the Intel 13th Gen Core H performance mobile processor series.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i9-13900H with vPro Enterprise.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-13700H with vPro Enterprise.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) Core i7-13700H features 14 physical cores and 20 concurrent threads.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) CPU core layout comprises 6 Raptor Cove P-cores and 8 Gracemont E-cores.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) Core i7-13700H P-cores operate at a 2.4 GHz base and up to 5.0 GHz boost.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) Core i7-13700H E-cores operate at a 1.8 GHz base and up to 3.7 GHz boost.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5200 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) CPU integrates Intel Iris Xe Graphics with 96 execution units operating at 1.50 GHz.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) GPU Specifications
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace professional workstation microarchitecture.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) flagship features an NVIDIA RTX 4000 Ada Generation Laptop GPU with 7,424 CUDA cores.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) tier-two features an NVIDIA RTX 3000 Ada Generation Laptop GPU with 4,608 CUDA cores.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) base features an NVIDIA RTX 2000 Ada Generation Laptop GPU with 3,072 CUDA cores.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) RTX 4000 Ada config equips 12 GB of GDDR6 VRAM across a 192-bit memory bus.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) RTX 4000 Ada delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 432 GB/s at 18 Gbps effective speed.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) discrete GPU operates at a configured 60 W base TGP plus 20 W Dynamic Boost.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 80 W TGP.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) delivers Independent Software Vendor (ISV) certifications for Maya, Premiere Pro, and AutoCAD.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 and enterprise OptiX ray tracing compute architectures.

## 5. HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) AI and Performance
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) does not incorporate a dedicated silicon NPU on its Intel 13th Gen processor.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) RTX 4000 Ada discrete GPU delivers up to 486 INT8 TOPS of dedicated AI compute performance.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) RTX 4000 Ada discrete GPU achieves up to 25.8 TFLOPS of FP32 compute performance.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) does not qualify for Microsoft Copilot+ PC certification due to lacking a 40 TOPS NPU.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) discrete GPU incorporates dual 8th-generation NVENC hardware video encoders with AV1 support.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) discrete GPU includes a 5th-generation NVDEC engine supporting 8K 60 fps AV1 and HEVC decoding.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) Core i7-13700H achieves approximately 16,500 points in Cinebench R23 multi-core benchmark testing.

## 6. HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) Power, Thermals and Cooling
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) CPU is configured with a 45 W sustained PL1 and 115 W peak PL2 turbo limit.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) sustains a maximum continuous combined workstation power dissipation of 110 W in Performance mode.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) cooling system incorporates HP Vaporforce thermals with a full vapor chamber and dual curved fans.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) applies high-grade thermal interface material across both enterprise processor packages.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) provides Optimized, Cool, Quiet, and Performance thermal profiles via HP Power Manager.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) integrates an expansive 6-cell 86 Wh lithium-ion Long Life rechargeable battery pack.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) ships with a compact 150 W or 200 W slim barrel AC power adapter.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) supports HP Fast Charge technology, reaching 50 percent charge in 30 minutes.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) triggers thermal safety throttling when GPU temperatures hit 87 °C or CPU exceeds 95 °C.

- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) features one USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C port and one USB 3.2 Gen 1 Type-A port.

- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) features a 720p HD IR camera with temporal noise reduction and Windows Hello facial login.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) provides enterprise wireless networking via Intel Wi-Fi 6E AX211 and Bluetooth 5.3.

- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) requires UEFI BIOS version F.12 or newer with enterprise HP Wolf Security and Sure Start.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) requires NVIDIA Production Branch/Enterprise Driver 535 or newer for ISV certified stability.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) officially supports 64-bit Microsoft Windows 11 Pro and enterprise Red Hat/Ubuntu Linux.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) memory is fully upgradeable via two internal DDR5 SO-DIMM slots supporting up to 64 GB.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) internal storage is user-upgradeable via a single internal M.2 2280 PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD slot.
- The HP ZBook Studio G10 (2023) includes a standard 3-year HP limited enterprise workstation warranty with onsite service.
