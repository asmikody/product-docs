# HP Pavilion 15 (2023) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP Pavilion 15 (2023) Overview and Design
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) officially launched in April 2023 as an everyday mainstream consumer laptop.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) belongs to the 15.6-inch budget-to-midrange everyday consumer laptop class.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) chassis features an aluminum keyboard deck with post-consumer recycled plastic base.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.75 kg.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) measures an accessible chassis profile thickness of 17.9 mm.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) targets university students, home office workers, casual web browsers, and budget shoppers.

## 2. HP Pavilion 15 (2023) Configurations
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) base config equips Core i5-1335U, Intel Iris Xe, 8 GB RAM, 256 GB SSD, FHD IPS.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) mid-tier config adds an Intel Core i7-1355U processor and 16 GB DDR4 RAM.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) storage config adds a 1 TB PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD and a touch-enabled FHD screen.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) flagship config adds an Intel Core i7-1355U, 16 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD, FHD touch display.

## 3. HP Pavilion 15 (2023) CPU Specifications
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-U low-power mobile microarchitecture.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) CPU utilizes the Intel 13th Gen Core U-series mobile processor lineup.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-1355U.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i5-1335U.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) Core i7-1355U features 10 physical cores and 12 concurrent threads.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) CPU core layout comprises 2 Raptor Cove P-cores and 8 Gracemont E-cores.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) Core i7-1355U P-cores operate at a 1.7 GHz base and up to 5.0 GHz boost.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) Core i7-1355U E-cores operate at a 1.2 GHz base and up to 3.7 GHz boost.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR4-3200 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) CPU integrates Intel Iris Xe Graphics with 96 execution units operating at 1.50 GHz.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) CPU integrates high-speed USB host controllers for external peripherals.

## 4. HP Pavilion 15 (2023) GPU Specifications
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) graphics system utilizes the integrated Intel Iris Xe Graphics processor.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) GPU silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) Iris Xe graphics silicon integrates 96 execution units and 768 shading cores.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) GPU dynamically shares up to 8 GB of dual-channel system DDR4 memory as VRAM.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) memory bus delivers up to 51.2 GB/s bandwidth to the integrated graphics core.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) integrated graphics processor operates at a peak boost frequency of 1.50 GHz.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) integrated GPU operates within a shared 15 W nominal processor power envelope.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) delivers reliable graphics processing for video streaming, office productivity, and dual monitors.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) GPU supports DirectX 12.1, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) GPU supports Intel oneAPI Level Zero compute architecture, while CUDA is unsupported.

## 5. HP Pavilion 15 (2023) AI and Performance
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) does not incorporate a dedicated silicon NPU on its Intel 13th Gen processor.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) CPU incorporates Intel Deep Learning Boost for accelerated AI inference workloads.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) integrated Intel Iris Xe GPU achieves up to 2.3 TFLOPS of FP32 compute performance.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) does not qualify for Microsoft Copilot+ PC certification due to lacking a 40 TOPS NPU.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) integrates Intel Quick Sync media engines supporting hardware HEVC and VP9 video decoding.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) media subsystem provides hardware acceleration for 4K 60 fps H.264 and HEVC video encoding.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) Core i7-1355U achieves approximately 8,100 points in Cinebench R23 multi-core benchmark testing.

## 6. HP Pavilion 15 (2023) Power, Thermals and Cooling
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) CPU is configured with a 15 W nominal PL1 and 28 W peak PL2 turbo limit.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) sustains a mainstream consumer thermal dissipation of 18 W under extended load.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) cooling system incorporates a single low-noise cooling fan with a single copper heatpipe.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) applies standard thermal grease interface material across the processor die.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) provides Cool, Quiet, and Performance thermal profiles via HP Command Center software.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) integrates a 3-cell 41 Wh lithium-ion rechargeable battery pack.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) ships with a standard 45 W or 65 W Smart AC power adapter.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) supports HP Fast Charge technology, reaching 50 percent charge in 45 minutes.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) initiates thermal protection throttling when CPU package temperature exceeds 95 °C.

- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) IPS panel delivers 250 nits brightness, 45% NTSC color gamut, and flicker-free DC dimming.

- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) includes a 3.5 mm combination headphone audio jack and dual stereo speakers tuned by B&O.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) features an HD 720p Wide Vision camera with integrated dual array digital microphones.
- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) provides wireless networking via Wi-Fi 6 dual-band 802.11ax and Bluetooth 5.3.

- The HP Pavilion 15 (2023) includes a standard 1-year HP limited consumer warranty with eligible Care Pack extensions.
