# HP Envy x360 16 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP Envy x360 16 (2024) Overview and Design
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) officially launched in March 2024 as a large-screen convertible productivity laptop.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) belongs to the 16-inch versatile 2-in-1 convertible entertainment and creator laptop class.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) chassis is crafted from Glacier Silver recycled all-aluminum metal alloy.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.91 kg.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) measures a slim convertible chassis profile thickness of 18.2 mm.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) targets creative enthusiasts, spreadsheet professionals, visual artists, and multimedia consumers.

## 2. HP Envy x360 16 (2024) Configurations
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) base config equips Core Ultra 5 125U, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, WUXGA IPS touch.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) mid-tier config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 155U processor and a 1 TB SSD.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) discrete config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 6 GB or RTX 4050 6 GB GPU.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) flagship config adds 32 GB LPDDR5 RAM, a 2 TB SSD, and a 2.8K 120Hz OLED touch display.

## 3. HP Envy x360 16 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake-U or Meteor Lake-H microarchitecture.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel Core Ultra 100U and 100H mobile processor series.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155U.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U features 12 physical cores and 14 concurrent threads.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) core layout comprises 2 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U P-cores operate at a 1.7 GHz base and up to 4.8 GHz boost.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U E-cores operate at a 1.2 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5-6400 MT/s memory.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Graphics with 4 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) integrated Intel GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 1.95 GHz.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) CPU integrates a native Thunderbolt 4 host controller providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. HP Envy x360 16 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace GPU microarchitecture.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) optional discrete config features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 Laptop GPU with 2,560 CUDA cores.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) RTX 4050 config equips 6 GB of dedicated GDDR6 VRAM across a 96-bit memory bus.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) RTX 4050 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 192 GB/s at 16 Gbps effective speed.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 45 W base TGP plus 10 W Dynamic Boost.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 55 W TGP.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) integrates NVIDIA Optimus automatic graphics switching with dynamic power management.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP Envy x360 16 (2024) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 compute architecture, while ROCm is unsupported on NVIDIA hardware.
