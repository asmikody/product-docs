# HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) Overview and Design
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) officially launched in January 2024 at CES 2024.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) belongs to the 16-inch ultra-slim flagship gaming and creator laptop class.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) chassis is crafted from magnesium-aluminum alloy in Ceramic White or Shadow Black finishes.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) carries a light flagship gaming weight of approximately 2.14 kg.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) measures a slim gaming profile thickness of 19.9 mm.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) targets hybrid gamers, digital creators, 3D artists, and creative professionals.

## 2. HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) Configurations
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) base config equips Core i7-14700HX, RTX 4060, 16 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD, WQXGA 240Hz.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 8 GB discrete GPU and 32 GB RAM.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) Mini-LED config adds a 240Hz 1,180 nits Mini-LED HDR 1000 display.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) flagship config adds an Intel Core i9-14900HX processor and an RTX 4070 GPU.

## 3. HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake Refresh-HX microarchitecture.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel 14th Gen Core HX enthusiast mobile processor series.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i9-14900HX.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-14700HX.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) Core i9-14900HX features 24 physical cores and 32 concurrent threads.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) CPU core layout comprises 8 Raptor Cove P-cores and 16 Gracemont E-cores.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) Core i9-14900HX P-cores operate at a 2.2 GHz base and up to 5.8 GHz boost.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) Core i9-14900HX E-cores operate at a 1.6 GHz base and up to 4.1 GHz boost.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) processor incorporates a massive 36 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) CPU integrates Intel UHD Graphics with 32 execution units operating at 1.65 GHz.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace GPU microarchitecture.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) flagship features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop GPU with 4,608 CUDA cores.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) base features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU with 3,072 CUDA cores.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) RTX 4070 GPU incorporates 144 4th-gen Tensor cores and 36 3rd-gen RT cores.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) RTX 4070 and RTX 4060 configs equip 8 GB of GDDR6 VRAM on 128-bit buses.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) RTX 4070 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 256 GB/s at 16 Gbps effective speed.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 115 W base TGP plus 15 W Dynamic Boost.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 130 W TGP.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) integrates NVIDIA Advanced Optimus automatic graphics switching with dedicated hardware MUX switch.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 compute architecture, while ROCm is unsupported on NVIDIA hardware.

- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) does not incorporate a dedicated silicon NPU on its Intel 14th Gen processor.

- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) discrete GPU incorporates an 8th-generation NVENC hardware video encoder with AV1 support.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) discrete GPU includes a 5th-generation NVDEC engine supporting 8K 60 fps AV1 and HEVC decoding.
- The HP Omen Transcend 16 (2024) Core i9-14900HX achieves approximately 29,800 points in Cinebench R23 multi-core benchmark testing.
