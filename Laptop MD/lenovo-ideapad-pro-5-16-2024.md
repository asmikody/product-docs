# Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) Buyer's Guide

- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) targets creative enthusiasts, software engineers, video editors, and university students.

- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) CPU belongs to the Intel Core Ultra 100H performance mobile series.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155H.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) base processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 5 125H.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H features 16 physical cores and 22 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) core layout comprises 6 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H P-cores operate at a 1.4 GHz base and up to 4.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H E-cores operate at a 0.9 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5X-7467 MT/s memory.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics featuring 8 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) integrated Intel Arc GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 2.25 GHz.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 16 (2024) CPU integrates a native Thunderbolt 4 host controller providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.
