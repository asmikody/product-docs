# Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) Overview and Design
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) officially launched in May 2023.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) belongs to the 16-inch maximum-performance desktop replacement mobile workstation class.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) chassis is constructed from storm grey aluminum and magnesium with red accent vents.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) carries a substantial workstation mass of approximately 2.95 kg.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) measures a heavy-duty chassis profile thickness of 30.23 mm.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) targets structural engineers, AI researchers, 3D simulation specialists, and VFX studios.

## 2. Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) Configurations
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) base config equips Core i7-13700HX, RTX A1000, 32 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, WUXGA IPS.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA RTX 2000 Ada Generation 8 GB GPU and 1 TB SSD.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) high-tier config adds an Intel Core i9-13980HX processor and an RTX 4000 Ada 12 GB GPU.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) flagship config adds an RTX 5000 Ada 16 GB GPU, 128 GB RAM, and 4K OLED touch.

## 3. Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) CPU Specifications
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-HX desktop-derived mobile microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) CPU utilizes the Intel 13th Gen Core HX enthusiast mobile processor series.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i9-13980HX.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-13700HX.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) Core i9-13980HX features 24 physical cores and 32 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) CPU core layout comprises 8 Raptor Cove P-cores and 16 Gracemont E-cores.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) Core i9-13980HX P-cores operate at a 2.2 GHz base and up to 5.6 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) Core i9-13980HX E-cores operate at a 1.6 GHz base and up to 4.0 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) processor incorporates a massive 36 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) memory controller supports quad SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s ECC or non-ECC memory.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) CPU integrates Intel UHD Graphics with 32 execution units operating at 1.65 GHz.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 and PCIe 5.0 lanes for up to two NVMe drives.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) GPU Specifications
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace workstation microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) flagship features an NVIDIA RTX 5000 Ada Generation GPU with 9,728 CUDA cores.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) tier-two features an NVIDIA RTX 4000 Ada Generation GPU with 7,424 CUDA cores.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) RTX 5000 Ada incorporates 304 4th-gen Tensor cores and 76 3rd-gen RT cores.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) RTX 5000 Ada equips 16 GB of GDDR6 ECC VRAM across a 256-bit memory bus.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) RTX 5000 Ada delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 576 GB/s at 18 Gbps effective speed.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) RTX 5000 Ada achieves a factory boost GPU clock speed of up to 2115 MHz.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) discrete GPU operates at a configured 115 W base TGP plus 15 W Dynamic Boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 130 W TGP.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) integrates a hardware MUX switch accompanied by discrete graphics BIOS routing.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) GPU supports ISV certified workstation drivers, DirectX 12 Ultimate, and Vulkan 1.3.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 compute architecture, while ROCm is unsupported on NVIDIA hardware.

- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) does not incorporate a dedicated silicon NPU on its Intel 13th Gen processor.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) RTX 5000 Ada discrete GPU delivers up to 653 INT8 TOPS of dedicated AI compute.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) RTX 5000 Ada discrete GPU achieves up to 41 TFLOPS of FP32 compute performance.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) does not qualify for Microsoft Copilot+ PC certification due to lacking a 40 TOPS NPU.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) discrete GPU incorporates dual 8th-generation NVENC hardware video encoders with AV1 support.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) discrete GPU includes a 5th-generation NVDEC engine supporting 8K 60 fps AV1 and HEVC decoding.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad P16 Gen 2 (2023) Core i9-13980HX achieves approximately 30,500 points in Cinebench R23 multi-core benchmark testing.
