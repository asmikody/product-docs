# HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) Overview and Design
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) officially launched in May 2023 as HP premier ultra-lightweight executive notebook.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) belongs to the 13.5-inch executive ultra-featherweight premium corporate laptop class.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) chassis is crafted from Slate Blue or Natural Silver CNC magnesium alloy.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) carries an ultra-featherweight starting device weight of approximately 0.99 kg.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) measures a remarkably slim executive chassis profile thickness of 16.4 mm.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) targets corporate executives, travelling consultants, board directors, and mobile enterprise leaders.

- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) base config equips Core i5-1335U, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, 3:2 WUXGA+ IPS.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) mid-tier config adds an Intel Core i7-1365U vPro processor and 16 GB RAM.

## 3. HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) CPU Specifications
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-U low-power mobile microarchitecture.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) CPU belongs to the Intel 13th Gen Core U-series vPro enterprise mobile lineup.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-1365U with vPro Enterprise.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i5-1335U with vPro Essentials.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) Core i7-1365U features 10 physical cores and 12 concurrent threads.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) CPU core layout comprises 2 Raptor Cove P-cores and 8 Gracemont E-cores.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) Core i7-1365U P-cores operate at a 1.8 GHz base and up to 5.2 GHz boost.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) Core i7-1365U E-cores operate at a 1.3 GHz base and up to 3.9 GHz boost.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5-6400 MT/s memory.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) CPU integrates Intel Iris Xe Graphics with 96 execution units operating at 1.50 GHz.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) graphics system utilizes the integrated Intel Iris Xe Graphics processor.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) GPU silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) Iris Xe graphics silicon integrates 96 execution units and 768 shading cores.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) GPU dynamically shares up to 16 GB of unified LPDDR5 system memory as VRAM.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) memory bus delivers up to 102.4 GB/s bandwidth to the integrated graphics core.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) integrated graphics processor operates at a peak boost frequency of 1.50 GHz.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) integrated GPU operates within a shared 15 W nominal processor power envelope.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) provides certified multi-display driving for enterprise Thunderbolt docking stations.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) GPU supports DirectX 12.1, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP Elite Dragonfly G4 (2023) GPU supports Intel oneAPI Level Zero compute architecture, while CUDA is unsupported.
