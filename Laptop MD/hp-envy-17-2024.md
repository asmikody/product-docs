# HP Envy 17 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP Envy 17 (2024) Overview and Design
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) officially launched in April 2024 as a large-format desktop replacement productivity laptop.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) belongs to the 17.3-inch premium desktop replacement consumer laptop class.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) chassis is crafted from Glacier Silver all-aluminum metal alloy with elevated hinge architecture.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 2.50 kg.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) measures an accessible desktop replacement chassis profile thickness of 19.9 mm.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) targets desktop replacement seekers, spreadsheet professionals, family organizers, and photo editors.

## 2. HP Envy 17 (2024) Configurations
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) base config equips Core Ultra 5 125H, Intel Arc, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, FHD touch.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) mid-tier config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 155H processor and a 1 TB PCIe 4.0 SSD.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) discrete config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 4 GB discrete GPU and 32 GB RAM.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) flagship config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 155H, 32 GB RAM, 2 TB SSD, 4K display.

## 3. HP Envy 17 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake-H performance microarchitecture.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) CPU belongs to the Intel Core Ultra 100H performance mobile series.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155H.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) base processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 5 125H.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H features 16 physical cores and 22 concurrent threads.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) core layout comprises 6 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H P-cores operate at a 1.4 GHz base and up to 4.8 GHz boost.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H E-cores operate at a 0.9 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics featuring 8 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) integrated Intel Arc GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 2.25 GHz.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) CPU integrates a native Thunderbolt 4 host controller providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. HP Envy 17 (2024) GPU Specifications

- The HP Envy 17 (2024) optional discrete GPU silicon is fabricated on Samsung 8 nm process technology.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) optional RTX 3050 Laptop GPU integrates 2,048 CUDA cores and 64 Tensor cores.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) RTX 3050 config equips 4 GB of dedicated GDDR6 VRAM across a 128-bit memory bus.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) RTX 3050 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 192 GB/s at 12 Gbps effective speed.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) integrated Intel Arc model dynamically shares up to 16 GB of DDR5 system RAM.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a conservative 45 W total graphics power envelope.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) supports multi-monitor productivity driving dual external 4K displays at 60 Hz.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP Envy 17 (2024) GPU supports Intel oneAPI Level Zero compute architecture on integrated models.
