# HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) Overview and Design
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) officially launched in March 2024 as HP top-tier flagship enterprise ultraportable notebook.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) belongs to the 14-inch executive flagship ultraportable corporate laptop class.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) chassis is crafted from CNC-machined Glacier Silver magnesium-aluminum alloy.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) carries a featherweight starting device weight of approximately 1.18 kg.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) measures an ultra-slim executive chassis profile thickness of 15.1 mm.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) targets corporate C-suite executives, senior consultants, board members, and enterprise leaders.

## 2. HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) Configurations
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) base config equips Core Ultra 5 125H, Intel Arc, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, WUXGA.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) mid-tier config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 155H vPro processor and 16 GB RAM.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) performance config adds 32 GB LPDDR5X RAM and a 1 TB PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) flagship config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 165H, 32 GB RAM, 2 TB SSD, 2.8K OLED.

## 3. HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake-H performance microarchitecture.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) CPU belongs to the Intel Core Ultra 100H vPro enterprise performance series.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 165H with vPro Enterprise.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155H with vPro Enterprise.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H features 16 physical cores and 22 concurrent threads.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) core layout comprises 6 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H P-cores operate at a 1.4 GHz base and up to 4.8 GHz boost.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H E-cores operate at a 0.9 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5x-7467 MT/s memory.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics featuring 8 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) integrated Intel Arc GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 2.25 GHz.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) graphics system utilizes the integrated Intel Arc Xe-LPG graphics processor.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) GPU tile is fabricated on TSMC 5 nm N5 process technology.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) Intel Arc graphics silicon integrates 128 vector engines and 8 hardware ray tracing units.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) GPU dynamically shares up to 16 GB of unified system LPDDR5x memory as VRAM.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) memory bus delivers up to 119.5 GB/s bandwidth to the integrated graphics core.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) integrated graphics processor operates at a peak boost frequency of 2.25 GHz.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) integrated GPU operates within a shared 28 W nominal processor power envelope.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) provides certified enterprise multi-display docking driving triple external displays.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP EliteBook 1040 G11 (2024) GPU supports Intel oneAPI Level Zero compute architecture, while CUDA is unsupported.
