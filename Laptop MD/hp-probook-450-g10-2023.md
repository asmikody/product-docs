# HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) Overview and Design
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) officially launched in April 2023 as a reliable mainstream business workforce laptop.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) belongs to the 15.6-inch essential commercial and small-medium business laptop class.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) chassis features an aluminum keyboard deck and top cover meeting MIL-STD-810H durability standards.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.79 kg.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) measures an accessible business chassis profile thickness of 19.9 mm.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) targets corporate fleets, accountants, small business owners, administrative teams, and schools.

## 2. HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) Configurations
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) base config equips Core i5-1335U, 8 GB RAM, 256 GB SSD, FHD IPS display.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) mid-tier config adds an Intel Core i7-1355U processor and 16 GB DDR4 RAM.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) discrete config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2050 4 GB discrete laptop GPU.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) flagship config adds 32 GB DDR4 RAM, a 1 TB SSD, and an FHD touch display.

## 3. HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) CPU Specifications
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-U low-power mobile microarchitecture.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) CPU utilizes the Intel 13th Gen Core U-series mobile processor series.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-1355U.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i5-1335U.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) Core i7-1355U features 10 physical cores and 12 concurrent threads.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) CPU core layout comprises 2 Raptor Cove P-cores and 8 Gracemont E-cores.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) Core i7-1355U P-cores operate at a 1.7 GHz base and up to 5.0 GHz boost.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) Core i7-1355U E-cores operate at a 1.2 GHz base and up to 3.7 GHz boost.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR4-3200 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) CPU integrates Intel Iris Xe Graphics with 96 execution units operating at 1.50 GHz.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) CPU integrates dual USB Type-C controllers supporting Power Delivery and DisplayPort.

## 4. HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) GPU Specifications
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) graphics system utilizes integrated Intel Iris Xe Graphics or an optional discrete GPU.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) optional discrete GPU is the NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2050 Laptop GPU.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) RTX 2050 discrete silicon integrates 2,048 CUDA cores and 64 Tensor cores.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) RTX 2050 config equips 4 GB of dedicated GDDR6 VRAM across a 64-bit memory bus.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) RTX 2050 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 112 GB/s at 14 Gbps effective speed.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) integrated graphics model dynamically shares up to 16 GB of DDR4 system RAM.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) discrete GPU operates within a power-efficient 30 W total graphics power rating.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) delivers reliable graphics processing for enterprise video conferencing and dual monitor setups.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP ProBook 450 G10 (2023) GPU supports Intel oneAPI Level Zero compute architecture on integrated models.
