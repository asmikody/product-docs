# HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) Overview and Design
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) officially launched in April 2024 as HP most portable ultra-mobile workstation.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) belongs to the 14-inch compact ultra-mobile entry workstation laptop class.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) chassis is crafted from Space Silver all-aluminum metal meeting MIL-STD-810H durability standards.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) carries a lightweight workstation device weight of approximately 1.45 kg.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) measures a slim ultra-mobile chassis profile thickness of 19.3 mm.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) targets mobile architects, civil engineers, GIS mapping specialists, and STEM educators.

## 2. HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) Configurations
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) base config equips Core Ultra 5 125H, Intel Arc, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, WUXGA.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) mid-tier config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 155H processor and 16 GB DDR5 RAM.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) discrete config adds an NVIDIA RTX A500 4 GB discrete workstation GPU.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) flagship config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 165H, 64 GB RAM, 2 TB SSD, 2.8K OLED.

## 3. HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake-H microarchitecture.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) CPU belongs to the Intel Core Ultra 100H vPro enterprise mobile processor series.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 165H with vPro Enterprise.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155H with vPro Enterprise.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H features 16 physical cores and 22 concurrent threads.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) core layout comprises 6 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H P-cores operate at a 1.4 GHz base and up to 4.8 GHz boost.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H E-cores operate at a 0.9 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics featuring 8 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) integrated Intel Arc GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 2.25 GHz.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ampere professional workstation microarchitecture.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) GPU silicon is fabricated on Samsung 8 nm process technology.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) discrete config features an NVIDIA RTX A500 Laptop GPU with 2,048 CUDA cores.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) RTX A500 GPU incorporates 64 2nd-gen Tensor cores and 16 2nd-gen RT cores.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) RTX A500 config equips 4 GB of dedicated GDDR6 VRAM across a 64-bit memory bus.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) RTX A500 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 112 GB/s at 14 Gbps effective speed.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 25 W base TGP plus 5 W Dynamic Boost.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 30 W TGP.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) delivers Independent Software Vendor (ISV) certifications for AutoCAD, MicroStation, and SolidWorks.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 and enterprise OptiX ray tracing compute architectures.

- The HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 (2024) integrates a dedicated Intel AI Boost NPU delivering up to 11 TOPS neural compute.
