- The Dell Inspiron 16 (5640) (2024) CPU core layout comprises 2 Raptor Cove P-cores and 8 Gracemont E-cores.

- The Dell Inspiron 16 (5640) (2024) Core 7 150U E-cores operate at a 1.2 GHz base and up to 4.0 GHz boost.
- The Dell Inspiron 16 (5640) (2024) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.

- The Dell Inspiron 16 (5640) (2024) CPU integrates Intel Graphics with 96 execution units operating at 1.30 GHz.

- The Dell Inspiron 16 (5640) (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Dell Inspiron 16 (5640) (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 NVMe storage.
- The Dell Inspiron 16 (5640) (2024) CPU integrates USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C controllers with Power Delivery and DisplayPort.
