# HP Envy 16 (2023) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP Envy 16 (2023) Overview and Design
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) officially launched in May 2023 as a creator and multimedia powerhouse.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) belongs to the 16-inch high-performance multimedia creator laptop class.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) chassis is crafted from Natural Silver sandblasted anodized all-aluminum metal alloy.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) carries an official device weight of approximately 2.34 kg.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) measures a sturdy creator chassis profile thickness of 19.9 mm.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) targets professional video editors, graphic designers, photographers, and audio engineers.

## 2. HP Envy 16 (2023) Configurations
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) base config equips Core i7-13700H, RTX 4060, 16 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD, WQXGA 120Hz.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 8 GB discrete GPU and 32 GB RAM.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) OLED config adds a 16.0-inch UHD+ 3840 x 2400 pixel 120Hz OLED touchscreen.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) flagship config adds an Intel Core i9-13900H processor, RTX 4080 12 GB, and 2 TB SSD.

## 3. HP Envy 16 (2023) CPU Specifications
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-H mobile microarchitecture.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) CPU utilizes the Intel 13th Gen Core H performance mobile processor series.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i9-13900H.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-13700H.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) Core i7-13700H features 14 physical cores and 20 concurrent threads.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) CPU core layout comprises 6 Raptor Cove P-cores and 8 Gracemont E-cores.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) Core i7-13700H P-cores operate at a 2.4 GHz base and up to 5.0 GHz boost.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) Core i7-13700H E-cores operate at a 1.8 GHz base and up to 3.7 GHz boost.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5200 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) CPU integrates Intel Iris Xe Graphics with 96 execution units operating at 1.50 GHz.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. HP Envy 16 (2023) GPU Specifications
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace GPU microarchitecture.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) flagship features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 Laptop GPU with 7,424 CUDA cores.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) tier-two features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop GPU with 4,608 CUDA cores.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) base features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU with 3,072 CUDA cores.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) RTX 4080 config equips 12 GB of GDDR6 VRAM across a 192-bit memory bus.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) RTX 4070 and RTX 4060 configs equip 8 GB of GDDR6 VRAM on 128-bit buses.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) RTX 4080 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 432 GB/s at 18 Gbps effective speed.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) discrete GPU operates at a configured 105 W base TGP plus 25 W Dynamic Boost.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 130 W TGP.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) integrates a hardware MUX switch accompanied by NVIDIA Advanced Optimus display switching.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 compute architecture, while ROCm is unsupported on NVIDIA hardware.

## 5. HP Envy 16 (2023) AI and Performance
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) does not incorporate a dedicated silicon NPU on its Intel 13th Gen processor.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) RTX 4080 discrete GPU delivers up to 486 INT8 TOPS of dedicated AI compute performance.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) RTX 4070 discrete GPU delivers up to 321 INT8 TOPS of dedicated AI compute performance.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) does not qualify for Microsoft Copilot+ PC certification due to lacking a 40 TOPS NPU.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) discrete GPU incorporates dual 8th-generation NVENC hardware video encoders with AV1 support.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) discrete GPU includes a 5th-generation NVDEC engine supporting 8K 60 fps AV1 and HEVC decoding.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) Core i7-13700H achieves approximately 16,800 points in Cinebench R23 multi-core benchmark testing.

## 6. HP Envy 16 (2023) Power, Thermals and Cooling
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) CPU is configured with a 45 W sustained PL1 and 115 W peak PL2 turbo limit.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) sustains a maximum continuous combined creator power dissipation of 150 W in Performance mode.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) cooling system incorporates dual high-airflow cooling fans with an expansive vapor chamber.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) applies liquid crystal polymer fan blades with four wide exhaust thermal vents.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) provides Smart Sense, Quiet, Cool, and Performance thermal profiles via HP Command Center.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) integrates an expansive 6-cell 83 Wh lithium-ion rechargeable battery pack.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) ships with a heavy-duty 200 W barrel AC power adapter.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) supports HP Fast Charge technology, reaching 50 percent charge in 30 minutes.
- The HP Envy 16 (2023) triggers thermal safety throttling when GPU temperatures hit 87 °C or CPU exceeds 95 °C.
