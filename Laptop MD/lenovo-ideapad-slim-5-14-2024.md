# Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) Overview and Design
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) officially launched in January 2024 at CES 2024.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) belongs to the 14-inch mainstream ultraportable consumer laptop class.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) chassis features an all-metal aluminum top and bottom lid with MIL-STD-810H durability.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.46 kg.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) measures a slim chassis profile thickness of 16.9 mm.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) targets university students, everyday office workers, remote employees, and commuters.

## 2. Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) Configurations
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) base config equips Core Ultra 5 125H, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, WUXGA OLED display.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) mid-tier config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 155H processor and a 1 TB SSD.

## 3. Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake-H microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) CPU belongs to the Intel Core Ultra 100H performance mobile series.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155H.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) base processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 5 125H.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H features 16 physical cores and 22 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) core layout comprises 6 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H P-cores operate at a 1.4 GHz base and up to 4.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H E-cores operate at a 0.9 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5X-7467 MT/s memory.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics featuring 8 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) integrated Intel Arc GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 2.25 GHz.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 NVMe storage.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) CPU integrates dual USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C controllers with DisplayPort and Power Delivery.

## 4. Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) graphics system utilizes the integrated Intel Arc Xe-LPG graphics processor.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) GPU tile is fabricated on TSMC 5 nm N5 process technology.

- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) memory bus delivers up to 119.5 GB/s bandwidth to the integrated graphics core.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) integrated graphics processor operates at a peak boost frequency of 2.25 GHz.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) integrated GPU operates within a shared 28 W nominal processor power envelope.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) delivers impressive graphical capability for photo editing, office multitasking, and casual gaming.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 14 (2024) GPU supports Intel oneAPI Level Zero compute architecture, while CUDA is unsupported.
