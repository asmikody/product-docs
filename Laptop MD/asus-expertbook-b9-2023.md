- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) CPU utilizes the Intel 13th Gen Core U enterprise mobile processor series.
- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-1365U vPro.
- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) base processor configuration is the Intel Core i5-1335U.
- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) Core i7-1365U features 10 physical cores and 12 concurrent threads.
- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) CPU core layout comprises 2 Raptor Cove P-cores and 8 Gracemont E-cores.
- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) Core i7-1365U P-cores operate at a 1.8 GHz base and up to 5.2 GHz boost.
- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) Core i7-1365U E-cores operate at a 1.3 GHz base and up to 3.9 GHz boost.
- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5-5200 MT/s memory.
- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) CPU integrates Intel Iris Xe Graphics featuring 96 execution units operating at 1.50 GHz.
- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 NVMe solid-state storage.
- The ASUS ExpertBook B9 (2023) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.
