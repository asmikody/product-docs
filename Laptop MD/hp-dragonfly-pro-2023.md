- The HP Dragonfly Pro (2023) processor incorporates 16 MB of shared L3 cache memory.
- The HP Dragonfly Pro (2023) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5-6400 MT/s memory.
- The HP Dragonfly Pro (2023) CPU integrates AMD Radeon 680M graphics based on RDNA 2 architecture.
- The HP Dragonfly Pro (2023) integrated Radeon 680M GPU features 12 compute units operating at up to 2.2 GHz.
- The HP Dragonfly Pro (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, AMD64, SSE4.2, and AVX2.

- The HP Dragonfly Pro (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The HP Dragonfly Pro (2023) CPU integrates dual USB4 40 Gbps Type-C controllers with Power Delivery and DisplayPort.
