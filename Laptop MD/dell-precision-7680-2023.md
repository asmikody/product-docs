# Dell Precision 7680 (2023) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Dell Precision 7680 (2023) Overview and Design
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) officially launched in April 2023 as Dell tier-one heavy-duty 16-inch mobile workstation.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) belongs to the 16-inch extreme enterprise desktop replacement mobile workstation class.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) chassis is crafted from Titan Gray CNC-machined aluminum with magnesium internal structural bracing.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) carries a robust workstation device weight of approximately 2.60 kg.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) measures an expandable workstation chassis profile thickness of 25.0 mm.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) targets machine learning engineers, cinematic animators, structural analysts, and geoscientists.

- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) base config equips Core i7-13850HX, RTX A1000, 32 GB CAMM RAM, 1 TB SSD, FHD+.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA RTX 3500 Ada 12 GB discrete GPU and 64 GB CAMM RAM.

## 3. Dell Precision 7680 (2023) CPU Specifications
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-HX mobile microarchitecture.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) CPU utilizes the Intel 13th Gen Core HX vPro enterprise mobile series.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i9-13950HX with vPro Enterprise.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-13850HX with vPro Enterprise.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) Core i9-13950HX features 24 physical cores and 32 concurrent threads.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) CPU core layout comprises 8 Raptor Cove P-cores and 16 Gracemont E-cores.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) Core i9-13950HX P-cores operate at a 2.2 GHz base and up to 5.5 GHz boost.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) Core i9-13950HX E-cores operate at a 1.6 GHz base and up to 4.0 GHz boost.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) processor incorporates a massive 36 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) memory controller supports innovative Dell CAMM DDR5 modules or SODIMM adapters up to 128 GB.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) CPU integrates Intel UHD Graphics with 32 execution units operating at 1.65 GHz.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 and PCIe 5.0 lanes for up to three NVMe SSDs.
- The Dell Precision 7680 (2023) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.
