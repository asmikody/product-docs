- The ASUS Vivobook S 14 OLED (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.

- The ASUS Vivobook S 14 OLED (2024) core layout comprises 6 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The ASUS Vivobook S 14 OLED (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H P-cores operate at a 1.4 GHz base and up to 4.8 GHz boost.
- The ASUS Vivobook S 14 OLED (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H E-cores operate at a 0.9 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The ASUS Vivobook S 14 OLED (2024) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The ASUS Vivobook S 14 OLED (2024) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5X-7467 MT/s memory.
- The ASUS Vivobook S 14 OLED (2024) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics featuring 8 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The ASUS Vivobook S 14 OLED (2024) integrated Intel Arc GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 2.25 GHz.
- The ASUS Vivobook S 14 OLED (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The ASUS Vivobook S 14 OLED (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The ASUS Vivobook S 14 OLED (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 NVMe storage connectivity.
- The ASUS Vivobook S 14 OLED (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.
