# Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) Overview and Design
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) officially launched in early 2025 as Lenovo premier corporate enterprise workhorse notebook.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) belongs to the 14-inch mainstream enterprise workforce business laptop class.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) chassis features recycled carbon fiber, magnesium, and aluminum in signature Thunder Black.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.36 kg.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) measures a slim commercial chassis profile thickness of 17.7 mm.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) targets enterprise fleets, corporate accountants, IT departments, and field professionals.

## 2. Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) Configurations
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) base equips Core Ultra 5 225H, Intel Arc, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, WUXGA IPS.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) mid-tier config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 255H vPro Enterprise processor.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) memory config adds dual SO-DIMM 32 GB DDR5-5600 MT/s RAM and a 1 TB SSD.

## 3. Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) CPU Specifications
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) CPU is built on the Intel Arrow Lake-H commercial mobile microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) CPU compute tile is fabricated on TSMC 3 nm N3B process technology.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) CPU utilizes the Intel Core Ultra 200H vPro enterprise mobile series.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 265H with vPro Enterprise.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 255H with vPro Enterprise.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) Core Ultra 7 255H features 16 physical cores and 16 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) core layout comprises 6 Lion Cove P-cores, 8 Skymont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) Core Ultra 7 255H P-cores operate at 2.4 GHz base and up to 5.1 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) Core Ultra 7 255H E-cores operate at 1.8 GHz base and up to 4.4 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s customer-replaceable memory modules.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics with 8 Xe2-cores based on Battlemage architecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) integrated Intel Arc GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 2.20 GHz.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 6 (2025) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bandwidth.
