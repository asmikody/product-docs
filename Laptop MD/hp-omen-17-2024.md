# HP Omen 17 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP Omen 17 (2024) Overview and Design
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) officially launched in April 2024 as the first AMD Hawk Point large-format Omen gaming laptop.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) belongs to the 17.3-inch desktop replacement mainstream gaming laptop class.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) chassis is crafted from Shadow Black post-consumer recycled plastics with an aluminum keyboard deck.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) carries a desktop replacement device weight of approximately 2.95 kg.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) measures a substantial gaming chassis profile thickness of 29.9 mm.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) targets competitive gamers, desktop replacement seekers, game modders, and multimedia power users.

## 2. HP Omen 17 (2024) Configurations
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) base config equips Ryzen 5 8645HS, RTX 4050, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, FHD 144Hz.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) mid-tier config adds an AMD Ryzen 7 8845HS processor and an RTX 4060 8 GB discrete GPU.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) performance config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 8 GB discrete GPU and 1 TB SSD.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) flagship config adds an AMD Ryzen 9 8945HS processor, 32 GB RAM, and a QHD 240Hz screen.

## 3. HP Omen 17 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) CPU is built on the AMD Zen 4 Hawk Point microarchitecture.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on TSMC 4 nm FinFET process technology.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) CPU belongs to the AMD Ryzen 8040 performance mobile processor series.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the AMD Ryzen 9 8945HS.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) primary processor configuration is the AMD Ryzen 7 8845HS.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) Ryzen 7 8845HS features 8 physical cores and 16 concurrent threads.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) Ryzen 7 8845HS operates at a 3.8 GHz base clock speed.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) Ryzen 7 8845HS achieves a maximum boost clock speed of up to 5.1 GHz.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) processor incorporates 16 MB of shared L3 cache memory.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) CPU integrates AMD Radeon 780M graphics based on RDNA 3 architecture.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) integrated Radeon 780M GPU features 12 compute units operating at up to 2.7 GHz.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, AMD64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and native AVX-512.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) CPU integrates USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C controllers with DisplayPort 1.4 output.

## 4. HP Omen 17 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace GPU microarchitecture.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) flagship features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop GPU with 4,608 CUDA cores.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) mid-tier features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU with 3,072 CUDA cores.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) base features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 Laptop GPU with 2,560 CUDA cores.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) RTX 4070 and RTX 4060 configs equip 8 GB of GDDR6 VRAM on 128-bit buses.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) RTX 4070 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 256 GB/s at 16 Gbps effective speed.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 115 W base TGP plus 25 W Dynamic Boost.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 140 W TGP.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) integrates NVIDIA Advanced Optimus automatic graphics switching with dedicated hardware MUX switch.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 compute architecture, while ROCm is unsupported on NVIDIA hardware.

## 5. HP Omen 17 (2024) AI and Performance
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) integrates a dedicated AMD XDNA NPU delivering up to 16 TOPS neural compute.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) RTX 4070 discrete GPU delivers up to 321 INT8 TOPS of dedicated AI compute performance.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) delivers 359 total platform TOPS across combined Zen 4 CPU, NPU, and RTX 4070 GPU.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) integrates the OMEN Gaming Hub AI optimizer for dynamic game performance tuning.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) does not qualify for Microsoft Copilot+ PC certification due to falling below 40 NPU TOPS.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) discrete GPU incorporates an 8th-generation NVENC hardware video encoder with AV1 support.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) Ryzen 7 8845HS achieves approximately 16,600 points in Cinebench R23 multi-core benchmark testing.

## 6. HP Omen 17 (2024) Power, Thermals and Cooling
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) CPU is configured with a 45 W sustained PL1 and 65 W peak PL2 turbo limit.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) sustains a maximum continuous combined gaming power dissipation of 165 W in Performance mode.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) cooling system incorporates OMEN Tempest Cooling with three-sided venting and dual 12V fans.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) applies high-grade thermal interface material across both processor packages.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) provides Eco, Quiet, Balanced, and Performance thermal profiles via OMEN Gaming Hub software.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) integrates a 6-cell 83 Wh lithium-ion rechargeable battery pack.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) ships with a standard 230 W barrel AC power adapter.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) supports HP Fast Charge technology, reaching 50 percent charge in 30 minutes.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) triggers thermal safety throttling when GPU temperatures hit 87 °C or CPU exceeds 95 °C.

## 7. HP Omen 17 (2024) Display, Ports and Connectivity
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) features a 17.3-inch QHD 2560 x 1440 pixel 16:9 240 Hz anti-glare IPS display.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) QHD panel delivers 300 nits brightness, 100% sRGB, 3 ms response time, and NVIDIA G-SYNC.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) alternate option provides a 17.3-inch FHD 1920 x 1080 pixel 144 Hz IPS panel with 300 nits.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) includes one USB Type-C 10 Gbps port supporting DisplayPort 1.4 and HP Sleep and Charge.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) features three USB Type-A 10 Gbps ports and a full-size HDMI 2.1 port.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) includes a dedicated 1 Gbps RJ-45 Ethernet jack and a 3.5 mm combination headphone jack.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) features an FHD 1080p camera with dual integrated microphones and manual privacy shutter.
- The HP Omen 17 (2024) provides wireless networking via Wi-Fi 6E dual-band 802.11ax and Bluetooth 5.3.

- The HP Omen 17 (2024) memory is fully upgradeable via two internal DDR5 SO-DIMM slots supporting up to 64 GB.

- The HP Omen 17 (2024) includes a standard 1-year HP limited hardware warranty with eligible Care Pack extensions.
