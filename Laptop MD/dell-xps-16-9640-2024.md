# Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) Buyer's Guide

- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) belongs to the 16.3-inch flagship performance multimedia creator laptop class.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) chassis is crafted from CNC-machined aluminum with Gorilla Glass 3 palmrest and capacitive touch row.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 2.13 kg.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) measures a slim performance profile thickness of 18.7 mm.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) targets creative directors, video editors, software developers, and executive power users.

- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) base config equips Core Ultra 7 155H, RTX 4050, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, FHD+ 120Hz.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 8 GB discrete GPU and 1 TB SSD.

## 3. Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) CPU Specifications
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake-H microarchitecture.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) CPU belongs to the Intel Core Ultra 100H performance mobile series.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 9 185H.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155H.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) Core Ultra 9 185H features 16 physical cores and 22 concurrent threads.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) core layout comprises 6 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) Core Ultra 9 185H P-cores operate at a 2.3 GHz base and up to 5.1 GHz boost.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) Core Ultra 9 185H E-cores operate at a 1.8 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5X-7467 MT/s memory.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics featuring 8 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) integrated Intel Arc GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 2.35 GHz.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) CPU integrates three native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace GPU microarchitecture.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.

- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) RTX 4070 and RTX 4060 configs equip 8 GB of GDDR6 VRAM on 128-bit buses.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) RTX 4070 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 256 GB/s at 16 Gbps effective speed.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) RTX 4070 operates at a configured 50 W base TGP plus 20 W Dynamic Boost.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 70 W TGP.
- The Dell XPS 16 (9640) (2024) integrates NVIDIA Advanced Optimus automatic display switching with dynamic GPU sleep states.
