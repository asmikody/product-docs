# Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) Overview and Design
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) officially launched in January 2024 at CES 2024.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) belongs to the 18-inch extreme desktop replacement gaming laptop class.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) chassis is constructed from Dark Metallic Moon anodized aluminum with structural magnesium bracing.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) carries a heavy-duty device weight of approximately 4.23 kg.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) measures a massive desktop replacement chassis profile thickness of 26.7 mm.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) targets competitive simulator gamers, extreme overclockers, VR developers, and 3D workstation engineers.

## 2. Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) Configurations
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) base config equips Core i7-14700HX, RTX 4070, 32 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD, QHD+ 165Hz.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 12 GB discrete GPU and Core i9-14900HX.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) flagship config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 16 GB discrete GPU and 64 GB RAM.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) extreme config adds quad M.2 SSD support up to 8 TB total storage in RAID 0.

## 3. Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake Refresh-HX microarchitecture.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel 14th Gen Core HX enthusiast mobile processor series.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i9-14900HX.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-14700HX.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) Core i9-14900HX features 24 physical cores and 32 concurrent threads.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) CPU core layout comprises 8 Raptor Cove P-cores and 16 Gracemont E-cores.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) Core i9-14900HX P-cores operate at a 2.2 GHz base and up to 5.8 GHz boost.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) Core i9-14900HX E-cores operate at a 1.6 GHz base and up to 4.1 GHz boost.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) processor incorporates a massive 36 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) CPU integrates Intel UHD Graphics with 32 execution units operating at 1.65 GHz.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 and PCIe 5.0 lanes for up to four NVMe storage drives.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace GPU microarchitecture.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) flagship features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 Laptop GPU with 9,728 CUDA cores.

- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) base features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop GPU with 4,608 CUDA cores.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) RTX 4090 GPU incorporates 304 4th-gen Tensor cores and 76 3rd-gen RT cores.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) RTX 4090 config equips 16 GB of GDDR6 VRAM across a 256-bit memory bus.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) RTX 4080 config equips 12 GB of GDDR6 VRAM across a 192-bit memory bus.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) RTX 4090 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 576 GB/s at 18 Gbps effective speed.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) RTX 4090 achieves a factory boost GPU clock speed of up to 2040 MHz.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 150 W base TGP plus 25 W Dynamic Boost.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 175 W TGP.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) integrates NVIDIA Advanced Optimus automatic graphics switching with dedicated hardware MUX switch.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The Dell Alienware m18 R2 (2024) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 compute architecture, while ROCm is unsupported on NVIDIA hardware.
