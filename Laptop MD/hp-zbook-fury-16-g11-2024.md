# HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) Buyer's Guide

- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) belongs to the 16-inch extreme enterprise desktop replacement mobile workstation class.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) chassis is crafted from Space Silver aluminum with internal magnesium alloy reinforcement.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) carries a heavy-duty workstation device weight of approximately 2.40 kg.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) measures an expandable workstation chassis profile thickness of 26.0 mm.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) targets data scientists, visual effects artists, deep learning researchers, and structural engineers.

## 2. HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) Configurations

- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA RTX 3500 Ada 12 GB discrete GPU and 64 GB RAM.

## 3. HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake Refresh-HX microarchitecture.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel 14th Gen Core HX vPro enterprise mobile series.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i9-14900HX with vPro Enterprise.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-14700HX with vPro Enterprise.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) Core i9-14900HX features 24 physical cores and 32 concurrent threads.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) CPU core layout comprises 8 Raptor Cove P-cores and 16 Gracemont E-cores.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) Core i9-14900HX P-cores operate at a 2.2 GHz base and up to 5.8 GHz boost.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) Core i9-14900HX E-cores operate at a 1.6 GHz base and up to 4.1 GHz boost.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) processor incorporates a massive 36 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) memory controller supports quad SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s ECC or non-ECC memory up to 128 GB.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) CPU integrates Intel UHD Graphics with 32 execution units operating at 1.65 GHz.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 and PCIe 5.0 lanes for up to four NVMe SSDs.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) RTX 5000 Ada GPU equips 16 GB of ECC GDDR6 VRAM across a 256-bit memory bus.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) RTX 5000 Ada delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 576 GB/s at 18 Gbps effective speed.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 115 W base TGP plus 15 W Dynamic Boost.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 130 W TGP.
- The HP ZBook Fury 16 G11 (2024) delivers full Independent Software Vendor (ISV) certifications for CATIA, Maya, Revit, and SolidWorks.
