# Dell G15 (5530) (2023) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Dell G15 (5530) (2023) Overview and Design
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) officially launched in March 2023 with continued mainstream availability throughout 2024.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) belongs to the 15.6-inch budget-to-midrange gaming laptop class.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) chassis features an angular retro 80s-inspired polymer design with contrasting thermal vents.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) carries an official device weight of approximately 2.81 kg.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) measures a thick gaming chassis profile thickness of 26.9 mm.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) targets budget gamers, college students, esports newcomers, and value-oriented PC users.

## 2. Dell G15 (5530) (2023) Configurations
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) base config equips Core i5-13450HX, RTX 3050 6 GB, 8 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, FHD 120Hz.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 6 GB discrete GPU and 16 GB RAM.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) performance config adds an Intel Core i7-13650HX processor and an RTX 4060 8 GB discrete GPU.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) flagship config adds 32 GB DDR5 RAM, a 1 TB SSD, and a 165Hz FHD display with 100% sRGB.

## 3. Dell G15 (5530) (2023) CPU Specifications
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-HX desktop-derived mobile microarchitecture.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) CPU utilizes the Intel 13th Gen Core HX enthusiast mobile processor series.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-13650HX.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) base processor configuration is the Intel Core i5-13450HX.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) Core i7-13650HX features 14 physical cores and 20 concurrent threads.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) CPU core layout comprises 6 Raptor Cove P-cores and 8 Gracemont E-cores.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) Core i7-13650HX P-cores operate at a 2.6 GHz base and up to 4.9 GHz boost.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) Core i7-13650HX E-cores operate at a 1.9 GHz base and up to 3.6 GHz boost.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-4800 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) CPU integrates Intel UHD Graphics with 16 execution units operating at 1.55 GHz.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) CPU integrates high-speed USB host controllers for external gaming accessories.

## 4. Dell G15 (5530) (2023) GPU Specifications
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace GPU microarchitecture.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) flagship features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU with 3,072 CUDA cores.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) mid-tier features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 Laptop GPU with 2,560 CUDA cores.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) base features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 6 GB Laptop GPU with 2,560 CUDA cores.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) RTX 4060 GPU incorporates 96 4th-gen Tensor cores and 24 3rd-gen RT cores.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) RTX 4060 config equips 8 GB of GDDR6 VRAM across a 128-bit memory bus.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) RTX 4060 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 256 GB/s at 16 Gbps effective speed.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) RTX 4060 achieves a factory boost GPU clock speed of up to 2370 MHz.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) discrete GPU operates at a configured 115 W base TGP plus 25 W Dynamic Boost.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 140 W TGP.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) integrates a hardware MUX switch accompanied by NVIDIA Advanced Optimus display switching.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 compute architecture, while ROCm is unsupported on NVIDIA hardware.

- The Dell G15 (5530) (2023) discrete GPU includes a 5th-generation NVDEC engine supporting 8K 60 fps AV1 and HEVC decoding.
