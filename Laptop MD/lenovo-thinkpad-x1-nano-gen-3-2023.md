# Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) Overview and Design
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) officially launched in April 2023.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) belongs to the 13.0-inch featherweight enterprise executive ultraportable laptop class.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) chassis features a hybrid carbon fiber top lid and magnesium-aluminum base.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) carries a featherweight starting mass of approximately 0.97 kg.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) measures an ultra-thin profile thickness of only 14.4 mm.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) targets globe-trotting executives, field auditors, consultants, and minimalist business professionals.

## 2. Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) Configurations
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) base config equips Core i5-1340P, 16 GB RAM, 256 GB SSD, 2K non-touch display.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) mid-tier config adds an Intel Core i7-1360P processor and a 512 GB SSD.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) vPro config adds an Intel Core i7-1370P vPro enterprise processor.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) flagship config adds 32 GB LPDDR5 RAM, a 1 TB SSD, and a 2K touchscreen.

## 3. Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) CPU Specifications
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-P microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) CPU belongs to the Intel 13th Gen Core P performance-thin mobile series.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-1370P vPro.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-1360P.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) Core i7-1360P features 12 physical cores and 16 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) CPU core layout comprises 4 Raptor Cove P-cores and 8 Gracemont E-cores.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) Core i7-1360P P-cores operate at a 2.2 GHz base and up to 5.0 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) Core i7-1360P E-cores operate at a 1.6 GHz base and up to 3.7 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) processor incorporates 18 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5-6400 MT/s memory.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) CPU integrates Intel Iris Xe Graphics featuring 96 execution units operating at 1.50 GHz.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for compact M.2 2242 NVMe storage.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) GPU Specifications
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) graphics system utilizes the integrated Intel Iris Xe graphics processor.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) GPU silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node within the processor.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) Intel Iris Xe silicon integrates 96 execution units and 768 shading units.

- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) memory bus delivers up to 102.4 GB/s bandwidth to the integrated graphics core.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) integrated graphics processor operates at a maximum dynamic frequency of 1.50 GHz.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) integrated GPU operates within a shared 20 W nominal processor power envelope.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) reliably powers dual external 4K displays through Thunderbolt 4 docking solutions.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) GPU supports DirectX 12.1, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Nano Gen 3 (2023) GPU supports Intel oneAPI Level Zero compute architecture, while CUDA is unsupported.
