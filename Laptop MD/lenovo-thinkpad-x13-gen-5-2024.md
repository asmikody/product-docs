# Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) Overview and Design
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) officially launched in March 2024.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) belongs to the 13.3-inch compact enterprise fleet workforce laptop class.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) chassis is built from carbon fiber reinforced polymer top and magnesium-aluminum base.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.17 kg.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) measures an ultraportable profile thickness of 15.95 mm.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) targets field technicians, enterprise analysts, public sector employees, and fleet deployments.

## 2. Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) Configurations
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) base config equips Core Ultra 5 125U, 16 GB RAM, 256 GB SSD, WUXGA display.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) mid-tier config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 155U processor and a 512 GB SSD.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) performance config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 155H processor with Intel Arc graphics.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) flagship config adds 64 GB LPDDR5X RAM, a 2 TB SSD, and a 2.8K OLED display.

## 3. Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV technology.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel Core Ultra 100U and 100H mobile processor series.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155U.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U features 12 physical cores and 14 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) core layout comprises 2 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U P-cores operate at a 1.7 GHz base and up to 4.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U E-cores operate at a 1.2 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5X-6400 MT/s memory.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Graphics featuring 4 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) integrated Intel GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 1.95 GHz.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) graphics system utilizes the integrated Intel Graphics 4 Xe-core processor.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) GPU tile is fabricated on TSMC 5 nm N5 process technology.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) Intel graphics silicon integrates 64 vector engines and 4 hardware ray tracing units.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) GPU dynamically shares up to 16 GB of unified system LPDDR5X memory as VRAM.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) memory bus delivers up to 102.4 GB/s bandwidth to the integrated graphics core.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) integrated graphics processor operates at a peak boost frequency of 1.95 GHz.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) integrated GPU operates within a shared 15 W nominal processor power envelope.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) delivers reliable graphics processing for video conferencing and office workflows.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X13 Gen 5 (2024) GPU supports Intel oneAPI Level Zero compute architecture, while CUDA is unsupported.
