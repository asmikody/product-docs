# Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) Overview and Design
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) officially launched in January 2024 at CES 2024 as the Yoga 9 2-in-1 Gen 9.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) belongs to the 14-inch luxury flagship consumer 2-in-1 convertible laptop class.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) chassis features precision CNC-machined aluminum with comfort-edge rounded polished contours.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.35 kg.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) measures a slim convertible profile thickness of 15.25 mm.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) targets creative enthusiasts, multimedia power users, digital artists, and executive consumers.

## 2. Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) Configurations
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) base config equips Core Ultra 7 155H, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, 2.8K OLED display.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) mid-tier config adds a 1 TB PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD option with Core Ultra 7 and 16 GB RAM.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) flagship config adds 32 GB LPDDR5X-7467 soldered RAM and a 4K 3840 x 2400 OLED touchscreen.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) bundles the magnetic Lenovo Slim Pen active stylus and a custom protective folio sleeve.

## 3. Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) CPU Specifications
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake-H microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) CPU belongs to the Intel Core Ultra 100H performance mobile series.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155H.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H features 16 physical cores and 22 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) core layout comprises 6 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H P-cores operate at a 1.4 GHz base and up to 4.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) Core Ultra 7 155H E-cores operate at a 0.9 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5X-7467 MT/s memory.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics featuring 8 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) integrated Intel Arc GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 2.25 GHz.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) GPU Specifications
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) graphics system utilizes the integrated Intel Arc Xe-LPG graphics processor.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) GPU tile is fabricated on TSMC 5 nm N5 process technology.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) Intel Arc graphics silicon integrates 128 vector engines and 8 hardware ray tracing units.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) GPU dynamically shares up to 16 GB of unified system LPDDR5X memory as VRAM.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) memory bus delivers up to 119.5 GB/s bandwidth to the integrated graphics core.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) integrated graphics processor operates at a peak boost frequency of 2.25 GHz.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) integrated GPU operates within a shared 28 W nominal processor power envelope.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) delivers impressive graphical capability for creative photo editing and light gaming.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) GPU supports Intel oneAPI Level Zero compute architecture, while CUDA is unsupported.

## 5. Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) AI and Performance

- The Lenovo Yoga 9i (2024) platform delivers up to 34 total TOPS across combined CPU, GPU, and NPU engines.
