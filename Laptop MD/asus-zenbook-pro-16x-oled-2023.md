# ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) Buyer's Guide

- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) chassis features CNC-machined aerospace-grade aluminum with an auto-tilting AAS Ultra keyboard mechanism.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) carries an official device weight of approximately 2.40 kg.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) measures a profile thickness ranging between 16.9 mm and 17.8 mm.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) targets professional video editors, 3D animators, colorists, and commercial visual artists.

- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-H microarchitecture.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) CPU utilizes the Intel 13th Gen Core H performance mobile processor series.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i9-13905H System-on-Module.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) base processor configuration is the Intel Core i9-13900H.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) Core i9-13905H features 14 physical cores and 20 concurrent threads.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) CPU core layout comprises 6 Raptor Cove P-cores and 8 Gracemont E-cores.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) Core i9-13905H P-cores operate at a 2.6 GHz base and up to 5.4 GHz boost.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) Core i9-13905H E-cores operate at a 1.9 GHz base and up to 4.1 GHz boost.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) processor incorporates 24 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) memory controller supports ultra-compact package-on-package soldered LPDDR5X-7467 MT/s memory.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) CPU integrates Intel Iris Xe Graphics with 96 execution units operating at 1.50 GHz.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 NVMe solid-state storage.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) discrete GPU operates at a configured 115 W base TGP plus 25 W Dynamic Boost.
- The ASUS Zenbook Pro 16X OLED (2023) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 140 W TGP.
