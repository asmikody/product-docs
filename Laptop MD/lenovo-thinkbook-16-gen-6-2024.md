- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) targets accountants, data analysts, office managers, and enterprise business staff.

## 3. Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-U and Raptor Lake-H microarchitectures.
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel 13th Gen Core mobile processor series.
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-1355U.
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) Core i7-1355U features 10 physical cores and 12 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) CPU core layout comprises 2 Raptor Cove P-cores and 8 Gracemont E-cores.
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) Core i7-1355U P-cores operate at a 1.7 GHz base and up to 5.0 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) Core i7-1355U E-cores operate at a 1.2 GHz base and up to 3.7 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5200 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Iris Xe Graphics featuring 96 execution units operating at 1.50 GHz.
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual M.2 NVMe storage.
- The Lenovo ThinkBook 16 Gen 6 (2024) CPU integrates a native Thunderbolt 4 host controller providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.
