# Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) Buyer's Guide

- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.65 kg.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) measures an accessible chassis profile thickness of 18.99 mm.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) targets budget shoppers, remote students, everyday home multitaskers, and light office workers.

## 3. Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) CPU Specifications
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-U low-power mobile microarchitecture.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) CPU utilizes the Intel 13th Gen Core U-series mobile processor series.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-1355U.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i5-1335U.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) Core i5-1335U features 10 physical cores and 12 concurrent threads.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) CPU core layout comprises 2 Raptor Cove P-cores and 8 Gracemont E-cores.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) Core i5-1335U P-cores operate at a 1.3 GHz base and up to 4.6 GHz boost.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) Core i5-1335U E-cores operate at a 0.9 GHz base and up to 3.4 GHz boost.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR4-2666 or DDR4-3200 MT/s memory modules.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) CPU integrates Intel Iris Xe Graphics with 80 execution units operating at 1.25 GHz.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 NVMe storage.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) CPU integrates high-speed USB host controllers for everyday peripherals.

- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) graphics system utilizes the integrated Intel Iris Xe Graphics processor.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) GPU silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.

- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) integrated graphics processor operates at a peak boost frequency of 1.25 GHz.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) integrated GPU operates within a shared 15 W nominal processor power envelope.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) delivers reliable graphics processing for video streaming, casual office tasks, and dual monitors.
- The Dell Inspiron 15 (3530) (2023) GPU supports DirectX 12.1, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
