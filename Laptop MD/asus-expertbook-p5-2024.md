# ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) Buyer's Guide

- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) belongs to the 14-inch next-generation enterprise Copilot+ AI PC laptop class.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) chassis features precision-machined aluminum with military MIL-STD-810H durability standards.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.29 kg.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) measures a slim chassis profile thickness between 14.9 mm and 16.4 mm.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) targets business owners, enterprise managers, mobile analysts, and security-focused professionals.

- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) P5405CSA base config equips Core Ultra 5 226V, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, 2.5K 144Hz.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) P5405CSA mid-tier config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 258V processor and 32 GB RAM.

## 3. ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Lunar Lake microarchitecture.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on TSMC 3 nm N3B process technology.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) CPU belongs to the Intel Core Ultra 200V low-power mobile processor series.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 258V.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) base processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 5 226V.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) Core Ultra 7 258V features 8 physical cores and 8 concurrent threads.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) CPU core layout comprises 4 Lion Cove P-cores and 4 Skymont low-power E-cores.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) Core Ultra 7 258V P-cores operate at a 2.2 GHz base and up to 4.8 GHz boost.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) Core Ultra 7 258V E-cores operate at a 2.2 GHz base and up to 3.7 GHz boost.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) memory controller integrates Memory-on-Package soldered LPDDR5X-8533 MT/s memory.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics 140V featuring 8 Xe2-cores based on Battlemage architecture.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) integrated Arc 140V GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 1.95 GHz.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel design choices.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for dual high-speed M.2 NVMe storage slots.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) graphics system utilizes the integrated Intel Arc 140V Battlemage graphics processor.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) GPU compute tile is fabricated on TSMC 3 nm N3B process technology.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) Intel Arc 140V integrates 8 2nd-gen Xe-cores and 8 hardware ray tracing units.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) GPU dynamically shares up to 16 GB of on-package LPDDR5X memory as VRAM.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) memory bus delivers up to 136.5 GB/s bandwidth to the integrated graphics core.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) integrated graphics processor operates at a peak boost frequency of 1.95 GHz.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) integrated GPU operates within a shared 17 W nominal processor power envelope.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) delivers 2x the integrated graphics efficiency of previous generation Meteor Lake GPUs.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The ASUS ExpertBook P5 (2024) GPU supports Intel oneAPI Level Zero compute architecture, while CUDA is unsupported.
