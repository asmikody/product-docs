## 3. Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) CPU Specifications
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) CPU is built on the Intel Lunar Lake microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) CPU compute tile is fabricated on TSMC 3 nm N3B process technology.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) CPU belongs to the Intel Core Ultra 200V low-power mobile processor series.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 9 288V.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 258V.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) Core Ultra 7 258V features 8 physical cores and 8 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) CPU core layout comprises 4 Lion Cove P-cores and 4 Skymont low-power E-cores.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) Core Ultra 7 258V P-cores operate at 2.2 GHz base and 4.8 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) Core Ultra 7 258V E-cores operate at 2.2 GHz base and 3.7 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) memory controller integrates Memory-on-Package soldered LPDDR5X-8533 MT/s memory.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) CPU integrates Intel Arc Graphics 140V featuring 8 Xe2-cores based on Battlemage architecture.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) integrated Arc 140V GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 1.95 GHz.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel design choices.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 5.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 NVMe storage drives.
- The Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 (2025) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.
