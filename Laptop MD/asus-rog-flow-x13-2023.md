# ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) Buyer's Guide

## 1. ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) Overview and Design
- The ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) officially launched in January 2023 at CES 2023.
- The ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) belongs to the 13.4-inch convertible 2-in-1 ultraportable gaming laptop class.
- The ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) chassis features an aluminum-magnesium alloy construction with a 360-degree geared hinge.
- The ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.30 kg.
- The ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) measures an ultra-thin profile thickness of 16.7 mm across the entire base.
- The ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) targets traveling professionals, graphic illustrators, and mobile gaming enthusiasts.

- The ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) Ryzen 9 7940HS operates at a 4.0 GHz base clock speed.
- The ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) Ryzen 9 7940HS achieves a maximum boost clock speed of up to 5.2 GHz.
- The ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) processor incorporates 16 MB of shared L3 cache memory.
- The ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) memory controller supports dual-channel LPDDR5-6400 MT/s soldered onboard memory.
- The ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) CPU integrates AMD Radeon 780M graphics based on RDNA 3 architecture.

- The ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, AMD64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and native AVX-512.
- The ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for internal NVMe storage connectivity.
- The ASUS ROG Flow X13 (2023) CPU integrates a native USB4 host controller supporting 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.
