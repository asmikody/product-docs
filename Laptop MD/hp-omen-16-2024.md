# HP Omen 16 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. HP Omen 16 (2024) Overview and Design
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) officially launched in January 2024 at CES 2024.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) belongs to the 16.1-inch high-performance mainstream gaming laptop class.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) chassis is crafted from Shadow Black aluminum lid with a reinforced polycarbonate keyboard deck and base.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 2.39 kg.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) measures a solid gaming profile thickness of 23.5 mm.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) targets competitive PC gamers, collegiate esports athletes, streamers, and STEM engineering students.

## 2. HP Omen 16 (2024) Configurations
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) base config equips Core i7-14700HX, RTX 4060, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, FHD 165Hz.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) mid-tier config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 8 GB discrete GPU and 1 TB SSD.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) performance config adds an Intel Core i9-14900HX processor and a QHD 240Hz display.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) flagship config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 12 GB GPU and 32 GB DDR5 RAM.

## 3. HP Omen 16 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake Refresh-HX microarchitecture.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel 14th Gen Core HX enthusiast mobile processor series.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i9-14900HX.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-14700HX.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) Core i9-14900HX features 24 physical cores and 32 concurrent threads.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) CPU core layout comprises 8 Raptor Cove P-cores and 16 Gracemont E-cores.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) Core i9-14900HX P-cores operate at a 2.2 GHz base and up to 5.8 GHz boost.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) Core i9-14900HX E-cores operate at a 1.6 GHz base and up to 4.1 GHz boost.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) processor incorporates a massive 36 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) CPU integrates Intel UHD Graphics with 32 execution units operating at 1.65 GHz.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. HP Omen 16 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace GPU microarchitecture.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) flagship features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 Laptop GPU with 7,424 CUDA cores.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) tier-two features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop GPU with 4,608 CUDA cores.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) base features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU with 3,072 CUDA cores.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) RTX 4080 config equips 12 GB of GDDR6 VRAM across a 192-bit memory bus.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) RTX 4070 and RTX 4060 configs equip 8 GB of GDDR6 VRAM on 128-bit buses.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) RTX 4080 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 432 GB/s at 18 Gbps effective speed.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 115 W base TGP plus 25 W Dynamic Boost.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 145 W TGP on RTX 4080 models.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) integrates NVIDIA Advanced Optimus automatic graphics switching with dedicated hardware MUX switch.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 compute architecture, while ROCm is unsupported on NVIDIA hardware.

## 5. HP Omen 16 (2024) AI and Performance
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) does not incorporate a dedicated silicon NPU on its Intel 14th Gen processor.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) RTX 4080 discrete GPU delivers up to 486 INT8 TOPS of dedicated AI compute performance.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) integrates the OMEN Gaming Hub AI optimizer for dynamic fan curves and voltage control.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) does not qualify for Microsoft Copilot+ PC certification due to lacking a 40 TOPS NPU.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) discrete GPU incorporates dual 8th-generation NVENC hardware video encoders with AV1 support.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) discrete GPU includes a 5th-generation NVDEC engine supporting 8K 60 fps AV1 and HEVC decoding.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) Core i9-14900HX achieves approximately 30,500 points in Cinebench R23 multi-core benchmark testing.

## 6. HP Omen 16 (2024) Power, Thermals and Cooling
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) CPU is configured with a 55 W sustained PL1 and 140 W peak PL2 turbo limit.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) sustains a maximum continuous combined gaming power dissipation of 170 W in Performance mode.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) cooling system incorporates OMEN Tempest Cooling with dual 12V fans and six heatpipe channels.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) applies high-grade thermal grease interface material across both processor dies.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) provides Eco, Quiet, Balanced, and Performance thermal profiles via OMEN Gaming Hub software.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) integrates an expansive 6-cell 83 Wh lithium-ion rechargeable battery pack.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) ships with a standard 230 W or 280 W barrel AC power adapter.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) supports HP Fast Charge technology, reaching 50 percent charge in 30 minutes.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) triggers thermal safety throttling when GPU temperatures hit 87 °C or CPU exceeds 95 °C.

## 7. HP Omen 16 (2024) Display, Ports and Connectivity
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) features a 16.1-inch QHD 2560 x 1440 pixel 16:9 240 Hz anti-glare IPS display.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) QHD panel delivers 300 nits brightness, 100% sRGB, 3 ms response time, and NVIDIA G-SYNC.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) alternate option provides a 16.1-inch FHD 1920 x 1080 pixel 165 Hz IPS panel with 100% sRGB.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) includes two Thunderbolt 4 Type-C ports supporting 40 Gbps data, DisplayPort, and charging.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) features three USB 3.2 Gen 1 Type-A ports and a full-size HDMI 2.1 port.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) includes a dedicated 1 Gbps RJ-45 Ethernet jack and a 3.5 mm combination audio jack.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) features an FHD 1080p camera with dual integrated microphones and manual privacy shutter.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) provides wireless networking via Intel Wi-Fi 7 BE200 and Bluetooth 5.4.

## 8. HP Omen 16 (2024) Compatibility and Constraints
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) requires UEFI BIOS version F.05 or newer for full hardware feature enablement.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) requires minimum display drivers of NVIDIA Game Ready Driver 551.23 or newer.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) officially supports 64-bit Microsoft Windows 11 Home and Windows 11 Pro operating systems.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) memory is fully upgradeable via two internal DDR5 SO-DIMM slots supporting up to 64 GB.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) internal storage is expandable via two M.2 2280 PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD slots.
- The HP Omen 16 (2024) includes a standard 1-year HP limited hardware warranty with eligible Care Pack extensions.
