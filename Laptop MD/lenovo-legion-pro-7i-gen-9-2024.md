- The Lenovo Legion Pro 7i Gen 9 (2024) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.

- The Lenovo Legion Pro 7i Gen 9 (2024) Core i9-14900HX features 24 physical cores and 32 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 7i Gen 9 (2024) CPU core layout comprises 8 Raptor Cove P-cores and 16 Gracemont E-cores.

- The Lenovo Legion Pro 7i Gen 9 (2024) Core i9-14900HX E-cores operate at a 1.6 GHz base and up to 4.1 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 7i Gen 9 (2024) processor incorporates a massive 36 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 7i Gen 9 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 7i Gen 9 (2024) CPU integrates Intel UHD Graphics with 32 execution units operating at 1.65 GHz.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 7i Gen 9 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 7i Gen 9 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 7i Gen 9 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 7i Gen 9 (2024) CPU integrates a native Thunderbolt 4 host controller providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.
