# Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) Overview and Design
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) officially launched in February 2024 at MWC 2024.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) belongs to the 15.6-inch mainstream business and enterprise workhorse laptop class.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) chassis is crafted from post-consumer recycled plastics and bio-based plastics.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) carries an official device weight of approximately 1.62 kg.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) measures an accessible enterprise profile thickness of 20.8 mm.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) targets corporate accountants, fleet enterprise buyers, administrative professionals, and data analysts.

## 2. Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) Configurations
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) base config equips Core Ultra 5 125U, 16 GB RAM, 256 GB SSD, FHD IPS display.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) mid-tier config adds an Intel Core Ultra 7 155U processor and a 512 GB SSD.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) discrete config adds an NVIDIA GeForce MX570A or RTX 2050 4 GB discrete GPU.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) flagship config adds dual SO-DIMM 64 GB DDR5 RAM and dual M.2 2 TB SSDs.

## 3. Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Meteor Lake-U and Meteor Lake-H microarchitectures.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) CPU compute tile is fabricated on the Intel 4 process node using EUV lithography.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel Core Ultra 100U and 100H mobile processor series.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core Ultra 7 155U with vPro.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U features 12 physical cores and 14 concurrent threads.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) core layout comprises 2 Redwood Cove P-cores, 8 Crestmont E-cores, and 2 LP E-cores.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U P-cores operate at a 1.7 GHz base and up to 4.8 GHz boost.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) Core Ultra 7 155U E-cores operate at a 1.2 GHz base and up to 3.8 GHz boost.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) processor incorporates 12 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) CPU integrates Intel Graphics featuring 4 Xe-cores based on Xe-LPG architecture.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) integrated Intel GPU operates at a maximum dynamic graphics frequency of 1.95 GHz.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and AVX-VNNI.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) CPU integrates dual native Thunderbolt 4 host controllers providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) graphics system utilizes integrated Intel Graphics or an optional discrete NVIDIA RTX 2050 GPU.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) optional discrete GPU silicon is fabricated on Samsung 8 nm process technology.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) optional RTX 2050 Laptop GPU integrates 2,048 CUDA cores and 64 Tensor cores.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) RTX 2050 config equips 4 GB of dedicated GDDR6 VRAM across a 64-bit memory bus.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) RTX 2050 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 112 GB/s at 14 Gbps effective speed.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) integrated graphics model dynamically shares up to 16 GB of DDR5 system RAM.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) integrated GPU operates within a shared 15 W nominal processor power envelope.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) supports multi-monitor enterprise workstation setups via dual Thunderbolt 4 docking stations.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The Dell Latitude 5550 (2024) GPU supports Intel oneAPI Level Zero compute architecture on integrated models.
