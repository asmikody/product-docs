# HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) Buyer's Guide

- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) officially launched in May 2024 reviving HP iconic OmniBook brand for the AI era.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) belongs to the 14-inch next-generation ARM Copilot+ ultraportable consumer laptop class.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) chassis is crafted from Meteor Silver or Ceramic White recycled aluminum alloy.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) carries a lightweight ultraportable device weight of approximately 1.35 kg.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) measures a slim consumer chassis profile thickness of 14.3 mm.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) targets college students, productivity multitaskers, creative hobbyists, and mobile professionals.

## 2. HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) Configurations
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) base config equips Snapdragon X Elite X1E-78-100, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, 2.2K touch.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) storage config adds a 1 TB high-speed PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD.

## 3. HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) CPU is built on the Qualcomm Oryon ARM64 mobile microarchitecture.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on TSMC 4 nm FinFET process technology.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) CPU belongs to the Qualcomm Snapdragon X Elite processor family.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Qualcomm Snapdragon X Elite X1E-78-100.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) Snapdragon X Elite features 12 custom Oryon physical compute cores.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) Snapdragon X Elite executes 12 concurrent threads without Simultaneous Multi-Threading.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) Oryon cores operate at a consistent multithreaded clock frequency of up to 3.4 GHz.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) X1E-78-100 processor model does not feature dual-core boost clocks.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) processor incorporates a massive 42 MB of total cache memory.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) memory controller supports dual-channel soldered LPDDR5X-8448 MT/s memory.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) CPU integrates a Qualcomm Adreno GPU delivering 3.8 TFLOPS compute.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) integrated Adreno graphics engine operates at a clock frequency of up to 1.25 GHz.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) CPU instruction support includes ARMv8.7-A 64-bit architecture with Prism x86 emulation.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed M.2 2280 NVMe storage.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) CPU integrates native USB4 40 Gbps host controllers with DisplayPort 1.4a.

- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) GPU compute silicon is fabricated on TSMC 4 nm process technology.

- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) GPU dynamically shares up to 16 GB of ultra-fast unified LPDDR5X memory as VRAM.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) memory bus delivers a massive unified bandwidth of 135 GB/s to the GPU core.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) integrated graphics processor operates at a boost frequency of up to 1.25 GHz.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) integrated GPU operates within an ultra-efficient shared 25 W nominal system power envelope.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) delivers smooth graphical rendering for creative photo editing and dual 4K external monitors.
- The HP OmniBook X 14 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12, Vulkan 1.3, and OpenCL 3.0 graphics APIs.
