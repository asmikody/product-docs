# Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) Buyer's Guide

- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) chassis features an Onyx Grey anodized aluminum top lid and rigid polymer base.

- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) measures a gaming chassis profile thickness of 26.9 mm.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) targets mainstream competitive gamers, engineering students, and video editors.

- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake Refresh-HX microarchitecture.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm process node.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel 14th Gen Core HX enthusiast mobile processor series.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) flagship processor configuration is the Intel Core i9-14900HX.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i7-14650HX.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) Core i7-14650HX features 16 physical cores and 24 concurrent threads.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) CPU core layout comprises 8 Raptor Cove P-cores and 8 Gracemont E-cores.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) Core i7-14650HX P-cores operate at a 2.2 GHz base and up to 5.2 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) Core i7-14650HX E-cores operate at a 1.6 GHz base and up to 3.7 GHz boost.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) processor incorporates 30 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) memory controller supports dual SO-DIMM DDR5-5600 MT/s replaceable memory modules.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) CPU integrates Intel UHD Graphics with 32 execution units operating at 1.60 GHz.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) CPU provides dedicated PCIe 4.0 lanes for high-speed dual NVMe storage.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) CPU integrates high-speed USB 3.2 Gen 2 host controllers for peripheral connectivity.

- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace GPU microarchitecture.

- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 115 W base TGP plus 25 W Dynamic Boost.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 140 W TGP.
- The Lenovo Legion Pro 5i Gen 9 (2024) integrates Advanced Optimus automatic graphics switching with dedicated hardware MUX switch.
