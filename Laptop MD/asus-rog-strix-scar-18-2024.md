# ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) Buyer's Guide

## 1. ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) Overview and Design
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) officially launched in January 2024 at CES 2024.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) belongs to the 18-inch flagship desktop replacement gaming laptop class.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) chassis combines an aluminum display lid with a semi-translucent composite gaming deck.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) carries a total device weight of approximately 3.10 kg.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) measures a profile thickness between 23.1 mm at the front and 30.8 mm at the rear.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) targets esports tournament competitors, heavy game developers, and workstation content creators.

## 2. ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) Configurations
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) G834JZR base config equips Core i9-14900HX, RTX 4080, 32 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD, 18-inch Mini-LED.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) G834JYR config adds an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 16 GB discrete laptop GPU.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) G834JYR tier-two config adds a 2 TB PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD with the RTX 4090 GPU.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) G834JYR flagship config adds 64 GB DDR5-5600 RAM and a 4 TB PCIe 4.0 RAID-0 SSD.

## 3. ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) CPU Specifications
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) CPU is built on the Intel Raptor Lake-HX Refresh microarchitecture.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) CPU compute silicon is fabricated on the Intel 7 10 nm Enhanced SuperFin process.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) CPU utilizes the Intel 14th Gen Core HX extreme performance mobile series.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) primary processor configuration is the Intel Core i9-14900HX.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) Core i9-14900HX features 24 physical cores and 32 concurrent threads.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) core configuration comprises 8 Raptor Cove P-cores and 16 Gracemont E-cores.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) Core i9-14900HX P-cores operate at a 2.2 GHz base and up to 5.8 GHz boost.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) Core i9-14900HX E-cores operate at a 1.6 GHz base and up to 4.1 GHz boost.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) processor incorporates 36 MB of shared Intel Smart Cache L3.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) memory controller supports dual-channel DDR5-5600 MT/s SO-DIMM removable modules.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) CPU integrates Intel UHD Graphics with 32 execution units operating at up to 1.65 GHz.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) CPU instruction support includes x86-64, SSE4.2, AVX2, and Intel DL Boost.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) CPU does not support AVX-512 instructions due to architectural Intel hybrid core parity.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) CPU connects to an Intel HM770 chipset providing extensive high-speed PCIe 4.0 lanes.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) CPU integrates a Thunderbolt 4 host controller providing 40 Gbps bidirectional bandwidth.

## 4. ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) GPU Specifications
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) discrete GPU is built on the NVIDIA Ada Lovelace GPU microarchitecture.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) GPU silicon is fabricated on a custom TSMC 4N NVIDIA process node.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) G834JYR features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 Laptop GPU with 9,728 CUDA cores.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) G834JZR features an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 Laptop GPU with 7,424 CUDA cores.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) RTX 4090 GPU incorporates 304 4th-gen Tensor cores and 76 3rd-gen RT cores.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) RTX 4090 config equips 16 GB of GDDR6 VRAM across a 256-bit memory bus.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) RTX 4080 config equips 12 GB of GDDR6 VRAM across a 192-bit memory bus.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) RTX 4090 delivers a maximum memory bandwidth of 576 GB/s at 18 Gbps effective speed.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) RTX 4090 achieves a factory ROG Boost GPU clock speed of 2040 MHz.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) discrete GPU operates at a configured 150 W base TGP plus 25 W Dynamic Boost.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) discrete GPU achieves a maximum total graphics power rating of 175 W TGP.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) integrates a hardware MUX switch accompanied by NVIDIA Advanced Optimus automatic display switching.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) GPU supports DirectX 12 Ultimate, Vulkan 1.3, OpenGL 4.6, and OpenCL 3.0 APIs.
- The ASUS ROG Strix Scar 18 (2024) GPU supports NVIDIA CUDA 12 compute architecture, while ROCm is unsupported on NVIDIA hardware.
