## Technical Context & Operational Architecture

The Apple iPhone 16 Plus shares its A18 silicon in full with the standard iPhone 16, meaning CPU and GPU performance ceilings are identical between the two, with the Plus model's larger chassis simply providing more room for a substantially bigger battery cell. The display driver engine governs a 6.7-inch Super Retina XDR OLED panel, sharing the same fixed 60Hz refresh rate as the smaller iPhone 16, reserving ProMotion exclusively for the Pro tier this generation.

Thermally, the Apple iPhone 16 Plus benefits from its larger internal volume, giving its internal heat-spreading layer more surface area to work with than the standard iPhone 16, resulting in marginally better sustained performance during longer workloads despite using identical silicon. The camera ISP pipeline mirrors the standard iPhone 16's dual 48MP Fusion array exactly, applying the same Photonic Engine and Smart HDR processing.

Structurally, the Apple iPhone 16 Plus uses an aluminum frame paired with Ceramic Shield front glass and a color-infused back glass panel, achieving an IP68 rating for dust and water resistance up to 6 meters for 30 minutes.
