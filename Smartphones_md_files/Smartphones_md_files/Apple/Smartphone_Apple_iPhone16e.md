## Technical Context & Operational Architecture

The Apple iPhone 16e is powered by a variant of the A18 chip configured with a 2-core GPU rather than the 5-core configuration found in the standard iPhone 16, a deliberate cost-reduction measure that still supports full Apple Intelligence functionality thanks to a preserved Neural Engine, but trades away meaningful graphics headroom for gaming and GPU-bound tasks. The display driver engine governs a 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED panel with a fixed 60Hz refresh rate, matching the standard iPhone 16's display specification exactly.

Thermally, the Apple iPhone 16e uses a simplified aluminum frame construction without a dedicated internal heat-spreading layer, appropriate given the trimmed-GPU chip's modest peak power draw. The camera ISP pipeline processes a single 48MP Fusion lens, the most significant hardware simplification versus the standard iPhone 16, reflecting the 16e's position as Apple's most affordable current-generation device. Notably, the Apple iPhone 16e debuts Apple's first in-house cellular modem, the C1, replacing the third-party modem used in every prior iPhone.

Structurally, the Apple iPhone 16e uses an aluminum frame paired with Ceramic Shield front glass and a glass back panel, achieving an IP68 rating for dust and water resistance up to 6 meters for 30 minutes, matching the durability of pricier models despite its budget positioning.

- At its core, the Apple iPhone 16e is powered by a 2-core GPU variant of the A18 chipset built on a 3nm process node.
