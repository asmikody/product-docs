## Technical Context & Operational Architecture

The Apple iPhone 16 is powered by the A18, a non-Pro variant that shares the same 3nm process node as the Pro tier's A18 Pro but implements a 5-core GPU rather than the Pro's 6-core configuration, while still supporting Apple Intelligence workloads at full capability thanks to a shared Neural Engine design. The display driver engine governs a 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED panel with a fixed 60Hz refresh rate, a deliberate cost-differentiation decision that keeps ProMotion as a Pro-exclusive feature for this generation.

Thermally, the Apple iPhone 16 uses an aluminum frame construction with a straightforward internal heat-spreading layer, well suited to the A18's more modest peak power draw relative to the Pro variant. The camera ISP pipeline processes a dual 48MP Fusion array, applying the same Photonic Engine and Smart HDR algorithms found across the 2024-era lineup, and this generation introduced the Camera Control button to the standard tier as well as the Pro tier.

Structurally, the Apple iPhone 16 uses an aluminum frame paired with Ceramic Shield front glass and a color-infused back glass panel, achieving an IP68 rating for dust and water resistance up to 6 meters for 30 minutes.
