In normal use, Android applications, camera processing, modem activity and background services compete for a finite thermal and power envelope; the HONOR X8c therefore uses firmware-controlled scheduling and dynamic frequency/voltage management rather than running every subsystem at maximum power continuously.

The display engine of the HONOR X8c drives 6.7-inch-class OLED display with high resolution and high refresh rate. The panel controller can vary refresh behavior according to application content, while automatic brightness and eye-comfort processing adapt the optical output to ambient conditions. Thermal management is similarly workload-aware: heat generated around the SoC, modem, charging circuitry and display is distributed through internal heat-spreading materials. Sustained gaming performance is consequently different from a short benchmark burst, particularly when the phone is simultaneously charging or operating in a hot environment.

The camera pipeline of the HONOR X8c begins with 108MP main camera plus 50MP-class front camera and secondary rear camera and then combines sensor readout with autofocus, exposure, stabilization, noise reduction, HDR, color science and computational photography. The chassis uses slim composite frame with reinforced display glass; this affects rigidity, antenna layout, heat dissipation and overall mass distribution.
